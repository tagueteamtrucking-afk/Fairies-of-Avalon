**Avalon Fairy 'Carol' Scaffold Plan**

1. **Objective**: Create a minimal scaffold for the Avalon Fairy 'Carol' character, focusing on key traits and story elements.

2. **Character Overview**:
   - Name: Carol
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Winter Solstice

3. **Physical Description**:
   - Height: 5'2"
   - Hair: Silver with hints of blue
   - Eyes: Icy blue
   - Wings: Delicate, translucent with frost patterns

4. **Personality Traits**:
   - Kind-hearted
   - Mysterious
   - Playful yet wise

5. **Backstory**:
   - Origin: Born from the first snowfall of winter
   - Purpose: To bring joy and magic during the winter season

6. **Powers and Abilities**:
   - Control over snow and ice
   - Ability to create winter-themed illusions
   - Healing touch with winter magic

7. **Key Relationships**:
   - Allies: Other seasonal fairies, woodland creatures
   - Enemies: Dark spirits that seek to disrupt the winter season

8. **Goals**:
   - Protect the spirit of winter
   - Spread joy and wonder during the holiday season

9. **Conflict**:
   - Struggles against a dark force threatening to steal the magic of winter

10. **Resolution**:
    - Unites with allies to restore balance and celebrate the beauty of winter.

This scaffold serves as a foundational guide for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Carol'.
