**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Sorcha'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Sorcha
   - Type: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, silver with hints of green
   - Eyes: Emerald green, sparkling

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years
   - Protective of her realm

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can control plants and animals
   - Healing Touch: Can heal wounds and ailments
   - Flight: Capable of swift, agile flight

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain balance in nature and protect the magical realm

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Foster harmony between magical creatures and nature
   - Defend against dark forces threatening her realm

7. **Allies and Relationships**
   - Close friends with woodland creatures
   - Mentor to younger fairies
   - Rivalry with dark fairies

8. **Challenges**
   - Confronting dark forces
   - Balancing her duties with personal desires
   - Navigating conflicts within the fairy community

9. **Visual Design Elements**
   - Color Palette: Greens, silvers, and soft pastels
   - Clothing: Ethereal, leaf-inspired garments
   - Accessories: Nature-themed jewelry (e.g., flower crowns, vine bracelets)

10. **Narrative Arc**
    - Introduction: Sorcha's daily life in Avalon
    - Conflict: The emergence of a dark force threatening nature
    - Climax: Sorcha's battle to protect her realm
    - Resolution: Restoration of balance and her growth as a guardian

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for the character Sorcha, allowing for further development and storytelling.
