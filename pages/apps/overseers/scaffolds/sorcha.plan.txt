**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Sorcha'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Sorcha
   - Role: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Kind, wise, playful
   - Appearance: Ethereal, glowing aura, delicate wings, flowing hair

2. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted forests of Avalon
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and protector of magical creatures
   - Key Events: Discovery of her powers, encounters with humans, and pivotal moments in her journey

3. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal wounds and ailments
   - Illusion Creation: Crafting illusions to protect or mislead

4. **Goals**
   - Protect Avalon from dark forces
   - Foster harmony between humans and magical beings
   - Discover her true potential and heritage

5. **Conflict**
   - Internal: Struggles with self-doubt and the weight of her responsibilities
   - External: Threats from dark creatures and human encroachment on Avalon

6. **Allies**
   - Other fairies: Friends and mentors
   - Magical creatures: Allies in her quest
   - Humans: A few who understand and respect the magic of Avalon

7. **Development Arc**
   - Growth: From a naive fairy to a wise protector
   - Challenges: Overcoming fears and learning to trust others
   - Resolution: Finding balance between her duties and personal desires

8. **Setting**
   - Avalon: Lush forests, sparkling rivers, hidden groves
   - Key Locations: Fairy glade, ancient tree, human village

9. **Themes**
   - Nature vs. Technology: The balance of magic and modernity
   - Friendship: The importance of connections and support
   - Self-Discovery: The journey to understanding one's true self

10. **Conclusion**
    - Sorcha's journey culminates in a decisive battle to protect Avalon, leading to her ultimate acceptance of her role as a guardian.
