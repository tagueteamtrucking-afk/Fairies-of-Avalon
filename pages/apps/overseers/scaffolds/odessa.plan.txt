**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Odessa'**

1. **Overview**
   - Name: Odessa
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Purpose: Enchantment and guidance

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wingspan: 10 inches
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels (lavender, mint green, pale pink)

3. **Core Features**
   - **Wings**: Gossamer, iridescent with intricate patterns
   - **Hair**: Long, flowing, shimmering silver
   - **Attire**: Ethereal dress made of flower petals and leaves

4. **Abilities**
   - **Magic**: Nature manipulation, healing, and light projection
   - **Communication**: Can speak with animals and plants

5. **Personality Traits**
   - Gentle, nurturing, wise, playful

6. **Habitat**
   - Enchanted forest with flowers, streams, and hidden glades

7. **Role in Story**
   - Guide for lost travelers, protector of nature, and mediator between humans and magical beings

8. **Goals**
   - To maintain harmony in the forest and assist those in need

9. **Supporting Characters**
   - Forest animals (friends and allies)
   - Other fairies (community members)

10. **Conflict**
    - Threats to the forest (e.g., pollution, deforestation)

11. **Resolution**
    - Collaboration with humans to restore balance and protect the environment

12. **Conclusion**
    - Odessa's journey emphasizes the importance of nature and kindness.
