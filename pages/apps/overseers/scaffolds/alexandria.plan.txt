**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Define the purpose and goals of Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'.

2. **Concept Development**
   - Outline the core themes and narrative elements.
   - Identify target audience and key demographics.

3. **Character Design**
   - Create character profiles for Alexandria and supporting characters.
   - Develop visual style and aesthetics.

4. **World Building**
   - Establish the setting and environment of Avalon.
   - Detail the magical elements and lore.

5. **Gameplay Mechanics**
   - Define core gameplay loop and player interactions.
   - Outline progression systems and rewards.

6. **Art and Sound Design**
   - Develop a visual style guide.
   - Create a soundscape and musical themes.

7. **Technical Requirements**
   - Identify platform(s) for release.
   - Outline software and tools needed for development.

8. **Timeline and Milestones**
   - Create a project timeline with key milestones.
   - Set deadlines for each phase of development.

9. **Testing and Feedback**
   - Plan for playtesting sessions.
   - Establish feedback collection methods.

10. **Marketing and Launch Strategy**
    - Develop a marketing plan leading up to launch.
    - Identify promotional channels and strategies. 

11. **Post-Launch Support**
    - Outline plans for updates and community engagement.
    - Define metrics for success and areas for improvement.
