**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Gossamer, iridescent
   - Hair: Silvery-blue, long and flowing
   - Eyes: Emerald green, sparkling

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Curious
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal minor wounds
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted forest of Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of the forest and its creatures

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the natural balance of Avalon
   - Foster harmony between fairies and other creatures

7. **Challenges**
   - Encroachment of dark forces threatening Avalon
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest animals
   - Elemental spirits

9. **Visual Design Elements**
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with hints of gold
   - Clothing: Ethereal dress made from flower petals and leaves

10. **Key Story Arcs**
    - Discovery of a dark force
    - Journey to unite the fairies
    - Overcoming personal doubts and fears

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Clarice, the Avalon Fairy.
