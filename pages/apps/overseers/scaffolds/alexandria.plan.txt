**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Create a magical and enchanting environment for the Avalon Fairy character, Alexandria.

2. **Setting**
   - Enchanted forest with vibrant flora and fauna.
   - A mystical pond with shimmering waters.
   - Twinkling lights and soft, ambient sounds.

3. **Character Design**
   - Appearance: Ethereal features, delicate wings, flowing gown in shades of blue and green.
   - Personality: Kind, wise, and playful.

4. **Key Elements**
   - **Home**: A tree hollow adorned with flowers and crystals.
   - **Companions**: Small woodland creatures (e.g., rabbits, birds).
   - **Magic**: Ability to communicate with nature and heal.

5. **Plot Points**
   - Introduction of Alexandria and her role in the forest.
   - A conflict involving a threat to her home (e.g., pollution, dark magic).
   - Resolution through teamwork with forest inhabitants.

6. **Themes**
   - Harmony with nature.
   - Friendship and collaboration.
   - The importance of protecting the environment.

7. **Visual Style**
   - Soft pastel colors with a focus on greens, blues, and purples.
   - Dreamy, whimsical illustrations.

8. **Target Audience**
   - Children aged 5-10 and families.

9. **Medium**
   - Illustrated storybook or animated short film.

10. **Timeline**
    - Concept Development: 1 month
    - Design and Illustration: 2 months
    - Production: 3 months
    - Marketing and Release: 1 month
