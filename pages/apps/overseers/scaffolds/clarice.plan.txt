**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Type: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise
   - Curious

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation
   - Flight
   - Healing Touch
   - Communication with Animals

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom in Avalon
   - Purpose: To protect the balance of nature and assist lost creatures

6. **Goals**
   - Maintain harmony in Avalon
   - Help others discover their true potential

7. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest animals
   - Elemental spirits

8. **Challenges**
   - Dark forces threatening Avalon
   - Misunderstandings with humans

9. **Development Arc**
   - Growth in confidence
   - Learning to confront fears
   - Strengthening bonds with allies

10. **Conclusion**
    - Clarice's journey leads to a deeper understanding of her powers and responsibilities as a guardian.
