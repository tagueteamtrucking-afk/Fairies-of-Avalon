**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Project Overview**
   - Name: Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'
   - Purpose: To create a whimsical and enchanting fairy character.

2. **Character Concept**
   - Appearance: Ethereal features, flowing hair, delicate wings, vibrant attire.
   - Personality: Curious, adventurous, kind-hearted, and playful.

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the mystical Avalon forest.
   - Journey: Explores the human world, spreading joy and magic.

4. **Key Features**
   - Wings: Translucent with iridescent colors.
   - Attire: Nature-inspired clothing adorned with flowers and leaves.
   - Accessories: Magical staff or wand, gemstone jewelry.

5. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Nature manipulation, healing, and light creation.
   - Flight: Swift and graceful movement through the air.

6. **Goals**
   - To inspire wonder and imagination in those she encounters.
   - To protect the balance of nature and magic.

7. **Visual References**
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with hints of gold and silver.
   - Inspiration: Traditional fairy folklore and modern fantasy art.

8. **Development Timeline**
   - Concept Art: 2 weeks
   - Character Design: 3 weeks
   - Finalization: 1 week

9. **Deliverables**
   - Character sketches
   - Full-color illustration
   - Background story document

10. **Review and Feedback**
    - Schedule: Weekly check-ins for progress updates and adjustments.
