**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Tracy'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Tracy
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wing Design: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leafy dress, adorned with flowers

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal minor wounds
   - Flight: Swift and agile in the air

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Protect the balance of nature and assist lost creatures

6. **Goals**
   - Maintain harmony in the Avalon ecosystem
   - Mentor young fairies in their magical abilities

7. **Challenges**
   - Threats from dark forces seeking to disrupt nature
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest creatures (e.g., birds, rabbits)

9. **Conflict**
   - Encounter with a dark sorcerer aiming to corrupt the forest

10. **Resolution**
    - Uniting with allies to restore balance and protect Avalon
