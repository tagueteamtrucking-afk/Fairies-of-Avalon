**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with floral patterns

3. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth
   - Healing Touch: Able to heal minor injuries
   - Flight: Capable of swift aerial movement

4. **Personality**
   - Friendly and approachable
   - Enjoys helping others
   - Has a mischievous side

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Journey: Explores the human world to learn and spread joy

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the natural world
   - Foster friendships with humans
   - Discover new magical abilities

7. **Challenges**
   - Navigating human misunderstandings
   - Balancing responsibilities in Avalon and the human realm

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy who guides Billie
   - Best Friend: A woodland creature who accompanies her

9. **Setting**
   - Avalon Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical creatures
   - Human World: Urban environments where Billie interacts with humans

10. **Plot Hooks**
    - A crisis in Avalon that requires Billie's intervention
    - A human discovering Billie's secret and the ensuing adventure

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Billie'.
