**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Avalon Grove

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Color Palette: Pastel greens and blues
   - Distinctive Features: Flower crown, shimmering skin

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and heal flora
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Illusion Creation: Can create visual illusions to protect the grove

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Mission: To maintain balance in the Avalon Grove and protect its secrets

6. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies of Avalon, woodland creatures
   - Enemies: Dark spirits seeking to corrupt the grove

7. **Goals**
   - Short-term: Protect the grove from immediate threats
   - Long-term: Ensure the survival and prosperity of Avalon’s magic

8. **Conflict**
   - Internal: Struggles with self-doubt about her abilities
   - External: Faces challenges from dark forces aiming to invade Avalon

9. **Development Arc**
   - Growth: Learns to embrace her powers and trust her instincts
   - Resolution: Unites the fairies and creatures of Avalon against a common foe

10. **Conclusion**
    - Legacy: Becomes a legendary figure in Avalon folklore, inspiring future generations of fairies.
