**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Enchanted Forest
   - Personality Traits: Kind, Curious, Playful

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors (lavender and pink)
   - Attire: Floral dress made of leaves and petals

3. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Communication: Can speak with animals and plants

4. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from a magical flower in the heart of the forest
   - Journey: Learned to harness her powers to protect her home

5. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect the Enchanted Forest from dark forces
   - Foster harmony between creatures and nature

6. **Allies and Relationships**
   - Friends: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who taught her magic

7. **Challenges**
   - Confronting dark spirits threatening the forest
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

8. **Development Arc**
   - Growth: From a carefree fairy to a wise protector
   - Key Events: Encounters with dark forces, forming alliances

9. **Conclusion**
   - Legacy: Ensuring the forest remains a sanctuary for all magical beings.
