**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base layer of gravel for drainage
   - Concrete footings for stability

2. **Main Structure**
   - Wooden frame construction (2x4 or 2x6 lumber)
   - Vertical posts at 4-foot intervals
   - Horizontal beams for roof support

3. **Walls**
   - Lightweight panels (plywood or composite)
   - Insulation between panels (if required)
   - Exterior cladding (wood, stone, or other materials)

4. **Roof**
   - Sloped design for rain runoff
   - Trusses for support
   - Roofing material (shingles, metal, or thatch)

5. **Windows and Doors**
   - Pre-framed openings for windows
   - Solid door frame with a weather seal
   - Glass or acrylic panels for windows

6. **Interior Layout**
   - Designated areas (living space, sleeping area, storage)
   - Basic furnishings (bed, table, chairs)
   - Lighting fixtures (solar or battery-operated)

7. **Utilities (if applicable)**
   - Basic plumbing (water source, drainage)
   - Electrical wiring (solar panels, batteries)

8. **Exterior Features**
   - Deck or porch area
   - Pathways (gravel or stone)
   - Landscaping (native plants, fairy garden elements)

9. **Safety and Compliance**
   - Ensure structural integrity
   - Adhere to local building codes
   - Fire safety measures (fire extinguisher, smoke detectors)

10. **Final Touches**
    - Paint or finish exterior
    - Decorate interior with fairy-themed elements
    - Install any additional features (fencing, lighting) 

**Note:** Adjust dimensions and materials based on specific site conditions and design preferences.
