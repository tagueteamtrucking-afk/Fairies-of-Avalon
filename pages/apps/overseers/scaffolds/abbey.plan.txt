**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Main Structure**
   - Frame: Wooden beams (2x4)
   - Walls: Plywood sheathing
   - Roof: A-frame structure with shingles

3. **Interior Layout**
   - Open floor plan
   - Designated areas: Living space, kitchen, sleeping nook

4. **Exterior Features**
   - Façade: Natural wood finish
   - Windows: Energy-efficient glass
   - Door: Rustic wooden door

5. **Utilities**
   - Electrical: Basic wiring plan
   - Plumbing: Simple water supply and drainage

6. **Landscaping**
   - Pathway: Stone or gravel
   - Garden: Native plants and flowers

7. **Safety Measures**
   - Railings on elevated areas
   - Fire safety equipment

8. **Finishing Touches**
   - Paint: Earthy tones
   - Decor: Fairy-themed elements

**Note:** Ensure compliance with local building codes and regulations.
