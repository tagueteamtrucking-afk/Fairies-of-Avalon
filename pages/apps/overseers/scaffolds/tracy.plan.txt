**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Tracy'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Tracy
   - Role: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Kind, playful, wise

2. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and guide for lost souls

3. **Appearance**
   - Features: Ethereal glow, delicate wings, flowing hair
   - Attire: Leafy dress adorned with flowers

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal wounds and ailments
   - Flight: Capable of flying gracefully

5. **Goals**
   - Protect Avalon: Maintain the balance of nature
   - Assist Others: Help those in need, especially lost travelers

6. **Challenges**
   - Dark Forces: Confronting threats to Avalon’s peace
   - Personal Growth: Learning to trust others and accept help

7. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Adversaries: Dark sorcerers, corrupted beings

8. **Key Themes**
   - Friendship: The importance of connections
   - Courage: Facing fears to protect what is loved

9. **Plot Points**
   - Discovery of a dark force threatening Avalon
   - Journey to unite allies and confront the threat
   - Personal revelation and growth through challenges faced

10. **Conclusion**
    - Restoration of peace in Avalon
    - Tracy’s evolution as a guardian and friend
