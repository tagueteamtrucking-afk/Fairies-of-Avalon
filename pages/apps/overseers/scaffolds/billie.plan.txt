**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Avalon Forest

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Flight
   - Nature manipulation (e.g., encouraging plant growth)
   - Healing touch

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain harmony in the forest and assist lost creatures

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the Avalon Forest from external threats
   - Help creatures in need
   - Discover the secrets of the forest

7. **Challenges**
   - Encroachment of human development
   - Rival fairies with different agendas
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

8. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other forest creatures and fairies
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who guides her
   - Antagonist: A human developer threatening the forest

9. **Story Arc**
   - Introduction: Meet Billie in her vibrant forest
   - Conflict: Discovery of the development plans
   - Climax: Confrontation with the developer
   - Resolution: Finding a way to protect the forest and its magic

10. **Themes**
    - Environmentalism
    - Friendship
    - Courage in the face of adversity
