**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors (lavender and mint green)
   - Attire: Ethereal dress made of flower petals and leaves
   - Accessories: Nature-themed jewelry (e.g., flower crown, vine bracelet)

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years
   - Protective of her realm

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing: Ability to heal wounds and ailments
   - Flight: Capable of swift and agile movement through the air

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Journey: Tasked with maintaining balance in nature and protecting the realm from dark forces

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Foster harmony between magical creatures and nature
   - Mentor younger fairies in their magical abilities

7. **Challenges**
   - Threats from dark entities seeking to corrupt Avalon
   - Balancing her duties with personal desires for adventure

8. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures, and nature spirits
   - Mentor: An elder fairy who guides her in her responsibilities
   - Rival: A dark fairy who opposes her ideals

9. **Setting**
   - Avalon: A mystical land filled with lush forests, sparkling rivers, and enchanted flora and fauna

10. **Story Arc**
    - Introduction: Clarice discovers a disturbance in nature
    - Conflict: Faces challenges from dark forces threatening Avalon
    - Resolution: Unites allies to restore balance and protect her home

This scaffold provides a foundational outline for the character of Clarice, the Avalon Fairy.
