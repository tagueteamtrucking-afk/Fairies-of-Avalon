**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective:**
   - Develop a fairy character named Alexandria with unique traits and abilities.

2. **Character Overview:**
   - Name: Alexandria
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

3. **Physical Attributes:**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair Color: Silver with hints of lavender
   - Eye Color: Emerald green

4. **Abilities:**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal minor wounds
   - Flight: Capable of swift and agile movement

5. **Personality Traits:**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Curious
   - Playful yet wise

6. **Backstory:**
   - Origin: Born in the Enchanted Forest
   - Role in the Fairy Realm: Protector of the forest and its creatures
   - Key Event: Saved the forest from a blight caused by dark magic

7. **Goals:**
   - Maintain balance in nature
   - Educate other fairies about environmental stewardship

8. **Allies:**
   - Forest creatures (e.g., rabbits, birds)
   - Other fairies in the Avalon realm

9. **Challenges:**
   - Confronting dark forces threatening the forest
   - Balancing her duties with personal desires

10. **Visual Design:**
    - Outfit: Flowing gown made of leaves and petals
    - Accessories: Flower crown, nature-themed jewelry

11. **Narrative Arc:**
    - Introduction: Alexandria's daily life in the forest
    - Conflict: Encountering a dark sorcerer
    - Resolution: Overcoming the sorcerer with the help of her allies

12. **Conclusion:**
    - Alexandria emerges as a stronger guardian, inspiring others to protect nature.
