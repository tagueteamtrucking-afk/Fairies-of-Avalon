**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Project Overview**
   - Name: Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'
   - Objective: Create a whimsical and enchanting fairy character.

2. **Character Design**
   - Appearance: 
     - Ethereal features, delicate wings, flowing attire.
     - Color Palette: Soft pastels with hints of iridescence.
   - Personality Traits:
     - Curious, kind-hearted, playful.

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the mystical Avalon forest.
   - Journey: Seeks to bring joy and magic to the human world.

4. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Control over nature, ability to communicate with animals.
   - Special Skills: Healing touch, creating illusions.

5. **Setting**
   - Environment: Enchanted forest with vibrant flora and fauna.
   - Key Locations: Fairy glen, crystal pond, ancient oak tree.

6. **Supporting Characters**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures.
   - Antagonists: Dark spirits, environmental threats.

7. **Plot Outline**
   - Introduction: Alexandria's life in Avalon.
   - Conflict: A disturbance in the natural balance.
   - Climax: Alexandria's quest to restore harmony.
   - Resolution: Triumph over adversity, strengthening of bonds with nature.

8. **Visual Elements**
   - Illustrations: Concept art for Alexandria and her environment.
   - Color Schemes: Mood boards reflecting the character's essence.

9. **Marketing Strategy**
   - Target Audience: Children and fantasy enthusiasts.
   - Platforms: Social media, children's literature, merchandise.

10. **Timeline**
    - Development Phases: 
      - Conceptualization (1 month)
      - Design & Illustration (2 months)
      - Marketing Launch (1 month)

11. **Budget Overview**
    - Estimated Costs: Design, marketing, production.

12. **Evaluation Metrics**
    - Success Indicators: Audience engagement, sales figures, feedback.
