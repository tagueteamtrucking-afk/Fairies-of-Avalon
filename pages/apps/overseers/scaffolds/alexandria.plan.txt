**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Develop a character profile and backstory for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'.

2. **Character Profile**
   - Name: Alexandria
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Age: 150 years (appears 25 in human years)
   - Appearance: Ethereal glow, iridescent wings, flowing silver hair, emerald green eyes.
   - Personality Traits: Curious, compassionate, wise, playful.

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the Enchanted Glade of Avalon, Alexandria was chosen by the Elder Fairies for her innate connection to nature.
   - Journey: Explored various realms, learning about different cultures and magic.
   - Purpose: Guardian of the Avalon Forest, protector of magical creatures, and keeper of ancient secrets.

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals.
   - Healing: Can heal wounds and ailments with her touch.
   - Flight: Swift and agile in the air, capable of high-speed travel.

5. **Goals**
   - To maintain harmony in Avalon and protect it from dark forces.
   - To discover the lost artifacts of Avalon that enhance her powers.

6. **Challenges**
   - Encounters with dark sorcerers seeking to exploit Avalon’s magic.
   - Balancing her duties with her desire for adventure and exploration.

7. **Allies and Enemies**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures, and ancient spirits.
   - Enemies: Dark sorcerers, corrupted magical beings, and environmental threats.

8. **Conclusion**
   - Alexandria’s journey is one of growth, learning, and the eternal struggle between light and darkness in Avalon.
