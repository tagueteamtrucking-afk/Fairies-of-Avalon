### Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'

#### 1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings for stability

#### 2. **Structure**
   - **Walls**
     - Lightweight timber framing
     - Insulated panels for energy efficiency
   - **Roof**
     - A-frame design for aesthetic appeal
     - Waterproof roofing material

#### 3. **Exterior**
   - Natural wood siding or eco-friendly composite
   - Decorative elements (e.g., fairy lights, carvings)

#### 4. **Interior Layout**
   - Open floor plan for communal space
   - Designated areas for sleeping, cooking, and relaxation

#### 5. **Utilities**
   - Basic plumbing for kitchen and bathroom
   - Electrical wiring for lighting and appliances

#### 6. **Landscaping**
   - Native plants and flowers for a fairy-tale ambiance
   - Pathways leading to and from the structure

#### 7. **Sustainability Features**
   - Rainwater collection system
   - Solar panels for energy independence

#### 8. **Safety Measures**
   - Fire exits and smoke detectors
   - Secure railings on elevated areas

#### 9. **Accessibility**
   - Ramps for wheelchair access
   - Wide doorways for ease of movement

#### 10. **Finishing Touches**
   - Interior decor with fairy-themed elements
   - Furnishings made from sustainable materials

This scaffold plan provides a basic framework for constructing Avalon Fairy 'Abbey' while ensuring functionality and aesthetic appeal.
