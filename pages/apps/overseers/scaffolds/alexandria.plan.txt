**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Title**: Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'

2. **Objective**: To create a whimsical and enchanting character that embodies the essence of Avalon, focusing on themes of nature, magic, and wisdom.

3. **Character Design**:
   - **Appearance**: Ethereal features, flowing hair, delicate wings resembling leaves.
   - **Color Palette**: Soft greens, blues, and hints of gold.
   - **Attire**: Flowing gown made of natural materials, adorned with flowers and vines.

4. **Backstory**:
   - **Origin**: Born in the mystical realm of Avalon, Alexandria is a guardian of nature and knowledge.
   - **Motivation**: To protect the balance of nature and guide lost souls.

5. **Abilities**:
   - **Nature Manipulation**: Control over plants and animals.
   - **Healing**: Ability to heal wounds and ailments using natural remedies.
   - **Wisdom Sharing**: Imparts knowledge and guidance to those in need.

6. **Setting**:
   - **Environment**: Lush forests, sparkling streams, and hidden groves within Avalon.
   - **Key Locations**: The Great Oak (a source of wisdom), the Crystal Lake (for healing), and the Enchanted Meadow (for gatherings).

7. **Supporting Characters**:
   - **Companions**: A wise owl and a playful fox.
   - **Allies**: Other fairies and magical creatures of Avalon.

8. **Conflict**:
   - **Antagonist**: A dark sorcerer seeking to exploit Avalon’s magic for personal gain.
   - **Challenge**: Alexandria must unite the creatures of Avalon to protect their home.

9. **Resolution**:
   - **Climax**: A battle of wits and magic against the sorcerer.
   - **Conclusion**: Restoration of balance in Avalon, reinforcing the importance of nature and wisdom.

10. **Themes**:
    - **Nature vs. Exploitation**: The importance of protecting the environment.
    - **Wisdom and Guidance**: The value of knowledge and mentorship.

11. **Visual Style**:
    - **Artistic Influence**: Inspired by classic fairy tales and nature illustrations.
    - **Medium**: Digital illustrations or watercolor.

12. **Target Audience**: Children and young adults who enjoy fantasy and adventure stories. 

13. **Potential Extensions**:
    - **Merchandise**: Plush toys, illustrated books, and art prints.
    - **Interactive Media**: Mobile app or game featuring Alexandria’s adventures.
