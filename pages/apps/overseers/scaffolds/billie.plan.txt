**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, kind-hearted

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Outfit: Floral dress with natural elements

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and harmony

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Healing Touch: Can heal small creatures and plants

5. **Goals**
   - Protect the Avalon forest from harm
   - Foster friendships with woodland creatures

6. **Challenges**
   - Threats from dark forces seeking to disrupt nature
   - Learning to balance her playful nature with responsibilities

7. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy who guides her
   - Friends: A mischievous squirrel and a wise owl

8. **Setting**
   - Avalon Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical elements

9. **Plot Points**
   - Discovery of a looming threat to the forest
   - Journey to gather allies and confront the danger
   - Personal growth and realization of her true potential

10. **Conclusion**
    - Restoration of harmony in Avalon
    - Billie’s evolution into a more responsible guardian
