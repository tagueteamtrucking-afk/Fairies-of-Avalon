**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings for stability

2. **Structure**
   - Wooden frame (2x4s) for walls
   - Roof trusses (pre-fabricated or built on-site)

3. **Walls**
   - Exterior: Plywood sheathing
   - Interior: Drywall or paneling

4. **Roof**
   - Asphalt shingles or metal roofing
   - Insulation between rafters

5. **Windows and Doors**
   - Pre-hung windows (energy-efficient)
   - Solid core door for main entrance

6. **Utilities**
   - Basic electrical wiring (outlets, switches)
   - Plumbing rough-in for kitchen and bathroom

7. **Finishes**
   - Interior paint or wallpaper
   - Flooring (laminate or vinyl)

8. **Exterior**
   - Siding (wood, vinyl, or composite)
   - Landscaping (minimal, native plants)

9. **Safety Features**
   - Smoke detectors
   - Fire extinguisher

10. **Inspection**
    - Ensure compliance with local building codes

**Note:** Adjust dimensions and materials based on specific site conditions and design preferences.
