**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Avalon

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years
   - Curious about the human world

4. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight: Agile and swift
   - Communication: Can speak to animals and plants

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the heart of Avalon
   - Purpose: Protect the balance of nature and guide lost souls

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Help those in need
   - Explore the human realm

7. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who taught her magic
   - Adversary: Dark spirit threatening Avalon

8. **Key Story Arcs**
   - Discovery of a hidden threat to Avalon
   - Journey to the human world for assistance
   - Learning to harness her full magical potential

9. **Themes**
   - Friendship and loyalty
   - The importance of nature
   - Courage in the face of adversity

10. **Conclusion**
    - Billie’s growth as a character through her adventures
    - The impact of her actions on Avalon and beyond
