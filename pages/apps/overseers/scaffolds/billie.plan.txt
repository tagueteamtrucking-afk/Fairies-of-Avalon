**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, kind-hearted

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 12 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with floral patterns

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Role: Guardian of nature and harmony
   - Motivation: To protect the balance of magic in Avalon

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Healing Touch: Can heal minor wounds of creatures

5. **Goals**
   - Protect Avalon from dark forces
   - Foster friendships with other magical beings
   - Explore the world beyond Avalon

6. **Challenges**
   - Encountering dark magic threatening Avalon
   - Learning to trust others outside her realm
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

7. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy who guides Billie
   - Friend: A mischievous sprite who often gets into trouble
   - Antagonist: A dark sorcerer seeking to corrupt Avalon

8. **Plot Points**
   - Discovery of a dark force in Avalon
   - Journey to gather allies
   - Confrontation with the antagonist
   - Resolution and restoration of harmony in Avalon

9. **Themes**
   - Friendship and trust
   - The importance of nature
   - Courage in the face of adversity

10. **Conclusion**
    - Billie learns valuable lessons about responsibility and teamwork, solidifying her role as a protector of Avalon.
