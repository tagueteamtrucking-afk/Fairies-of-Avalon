**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, Curious, Kind-hearted

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with floral patterns

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Role: Guardian of nature and harmony
   - Motivation: Protect the balance of the magical realm

4. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Communication: Can speak with animals and plants

5. **Goals**
   - Short-term: Help a lost creature find its way home
   - Long-term: Preserve the Avalon forest from external threats

6. **Conflict**
   - Internal: Struggles with self-doubt about her abilities
   - External: Faces a dark force threatening the Avalon realm

7. **Resolution**
   - Growth: Learns to trust her instincts and powers
   - Outcome: Successfully protects the forest and gains confidence

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who guides Billie
   - Friend: A mischievous sprite who provides comic relief
   - Antagonist: A shadowy figure seeking to corrupt the forest

9. **Themes**
   - Friendship: Importance of collaboration and support
   - Courage: Overcoming fears to protect what you love
   - Nature: Connection and respect for the environment

10. **Visual Elements**
    - Color Palette: Soft pastels with vibrant greens and floral accents
    - Setting: Lush, magical forest with sparkling elements

This scaffold plan outlines the essential components for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Billie'.
