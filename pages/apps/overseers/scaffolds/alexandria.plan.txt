**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Project Overview**
   - Title: Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'
   - Objective: Create a whimsical and enchanting fairy character inspired by the theme of Avalon.

2. **Character Design**
   - Appearance: Ethereal features, flowing garments, delicate wings.
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with hints of gold and silver.
   - Accessories: Nature-inspired jewelry, a staff or wand.

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the mystical land of Avalon.
   - Role: Guardian of knowledge and wisdom.
   - Personality Traits: Curious, wise, nurturing.

4. **Environment**
   - Setting: Enchanted forest with magical flora and fauna.
   - Key Locations: Fairy glade, ancient library, crystal pond.

5. **Interactions**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures, mystical beings.
   - Enemies: Dark spirits or forces threatening Avalon.

6. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Knowledge-based spells, nature manipulation.
   - Skills: Healing, wisdom sharing, flight.

7. **Narrative Arc**
   - Introduction: Alexandria's role and the beauty of Avalon.
   - Conflict: A threat to Avalon’s knowledge and harmony.
   - Resolution: Alexandria’s journey to restore balance and protect her home.

8. **Visual Elements**
   - Illustrations: Concept art for Alexandria and her environment.
   - Animation: Fluid movements for flight and magic use.

9. **Marketing**
   - Target Audience: Fantasy enthusiasts, children, and young adults.
   - Platforms: Social media, merchandise, storytelling events.

10. **Timeline**
    - Phase 1: Character design and backstory development (1 month).
    - Phase 2: Environment creation and narrative development (2 months).
    - Phase 3: Finalizing visuals and marketing strategy (1 month).

11. **Budget**
    - Estimate costs for design, marketing, and production.

12. **Evaluation**
    - Feedback mechanisms: Surveys, focus groups, and social media engagement.
