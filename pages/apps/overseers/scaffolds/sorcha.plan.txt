**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Sorcha'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Sorcha
   - Type: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Light

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Gossamer, iridescent
   - Hair: Silver with hints of lavender
   - Eyes: Bright emerald green
   - Attire: Flowing gown made of petals and leaves

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Light Manipulation: Can create and control light
   - Healing Touch: Can heal plants and creatures
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first light of dawn in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain balance between light and dark in her realm

6. **Allies**
   - Other Fairies: Collaborates with fellow guardians
   - Animals: Communicates with woodland creatures

7. **Challenges**
   - Dark Forces: Faces threats from shadow creatures
   - Personal Growth: Struggles with self-doubt

8. **Goals**
   - Protect Avalon: Ensure the safety and harmony of her realm
   - Discover True Potential: Learn to harness her powers fully

9. **Visual References**
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with vibrant greens
   - Inspiration: Nature, ethereal themes, and folklore

10. **Narrative Arc**
    - Introduction: Sorcha's daily life and duties in Avalon
    - Conflict: Encounter with dark forces disrupting the balance
    - Resolution: Overcoming challenges and embracing her powers

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the character Sorcha in a narrative or artistic context.
