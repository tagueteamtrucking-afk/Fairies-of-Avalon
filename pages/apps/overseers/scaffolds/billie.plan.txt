**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Setting**
   - Location: Enchanted Avalon Forest
   - Environment: Lush greenery, sparkling streams, magical flora

4. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from a rare flower in Avalon
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and harmony

5. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Nature manipulation, healing powers
   - Flight: Agile and swift

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the forest and its creatures
   - Foster friendship among magical beings

7. **Challenges**
   - Threats from dark forces
   - Balancing her playful nature with responsibilities

8. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Rivals: Dark spirits, pollution

9. **Development Arc**
   - Growth from playful fairy to a wise protector
   - Learning the importance of teamwork and sacrifice

10. **Conclusion**
    - Embraces her role as a leader in the Avalon community.
