**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Tracy'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Tracy
   - Role: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Kind, playful, wise

2. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and guide for lost souls

3. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Attire: Flowing dress made of leaves and flowers
   - Features: Bright green eyes, long flowing hair with floral accents

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing: Ability to heal wounds and ailments
   - Illusion: Create illusions to protect Avalon

5. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect Avalon: Ensure the safety of her home
   - Help Others: Assist those in need, especially lost travelers
   - Maintain Balance: Preserve harmony between nature and magic

6. **Challenges**
   - Dark Forces: Threats from malevolent beings seeking to disrupt Avalon
   - Personal Doubts: Struggles with her own insecurities about her abilities

7. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies and forest creatures
   - Mentor: An elder fairy who guides her
   - Friends: A group of adventurous humans who visit Avalon

8. **Key Plot Points**
   - Discovery of a dark force threatening Avalon
   - Journey to gather allies and strengthen her powers
   - Confrontation with the antagonist and resolution of the conflict

9. **Conclusion**
   - Restoration of peace in Avalon
   - Growth and acceptance of her role as a guardian

10. **Future Development**
    - Potential for new adventures and deeper exploration of her powers and relationships.
