**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Main Structure**
   - Frame: Wooden beams and columns
   - Walls: Lightweight panels (plywood or similar)

3. **Roof**
   - Trusses: Pre-fabricated wooden trusses
   - Covering: Waterproof membrane

4. **Access**
   - Staircase: Modular stairs for upper levels
   - Ladders: Portable access ladders

5. **Utilities**
   - Electrical: Basic wiring for lighting
   - Plumbing: Rough-in for water supply

6. **Safety Features**
   - Guardrails: Installed around elevated areas
   - Safety netting: Below work areas

7. **Finishing Touches**
   - Exterior: Natural wood finish or paint
   - Landscaping: Minimal, with native plants

8. **Inspection Points**
   - Structural integrity checks
   - Safety compliance verification

**Note:** Adjust dimensions and materials based on specific design requirements and local building codes.
