# Worldbuilding checklist (v1)

**Core**
- Premise & tone
- Magic/tech level + constraints
- Travel & geography (how people move)
- Factions, economies, conflict sources
- Languages & naming schemes
- Safety/maturity guidelines

**Culture**
- Values, taboos, holidays, cuisine, fashion
- Myths, religions, philosophies
- Law, justice, governance

**Systems**
- Magic rules (costs, risks, rarities) or tech rules (energy, logistics)
- Communication, education, healthcare
- Trade routes & money

**Story Tools**
- Starter hook & protagonist archetype
- Antagonist motivation
- 3‑act or 5‑beat outline
- Escalation ladder (stakes increase plan)

**Production**
- Art bible (palettes, silhouettes, motifs)
- Lexicon (terms, honorifics)
- Map + travel times
- Timeline of major events
