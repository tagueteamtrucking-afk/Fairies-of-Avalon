**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Clear and level the site
   - Lay a base of compacted gravel or concrete

2. **Structure**
   - Erect vertical supports (wooden or metal) for walls
   - Install horizontal beams for ceiling support

3. **Walls**
   - Frame walls with lightweight materials (plywood or drywall)
   - Include openings for windows and doors

4. **Roof**
   - Construct a simple gable or flat roof frame
   - Cover with waterproof material (shingles or metal)

5. **Access**
   - Build a staircase or ladder for entry
   - Ensure safety railings are installed

6. **Utilities**
   - Plan for electrical wiring and plumbing (if applicable)
   - Install insulation for temperature control

7. **Finishing Touches**
   - Add exterior cladding (wood, stone, or paint)
   - Landscape surrounding area for aesthetic appeal

8. **Safety Measures**
   - Ensure all materials are secured
   - Conduct regular inspections during construction

9. **Final Inspection**
   - Verify structural integrity and compliance with local codes
   - Obtain necessary permits and approvals before use
