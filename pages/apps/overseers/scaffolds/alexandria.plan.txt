**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Create a whimsical and enchanting environment for the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'.

2. **Theme**
   - Enchanted forest with elements of magic and nature.

3. **Materials Needed**
   - Lightweight wood or PVC for structure
   - Fabric (organza, tulle) for draping
   - LED fairy lights
   - Natural elements (twigs, leaves, flowers)
   - Paint (pastel colors)
   - Adhesives (glue, tape)

4. **Structure**
   - Base: Circular platform (4 ft diameter)
   - Vertical supports: 4 poles (6 ft tall) at equal intervals
   - Cross beams: Connect poles at the top (4 ft diameter)

5. **Draping**
   - Hang fabric from the top cross beams to create a canopy effect.
   - Use multiple layers for depth and texture.

6. **Lighting**
   - Install LED fairy lights along the top beams and within the fabric draping.

7. **Decorations**
   - Attach natural elements (twigs, leaves) to the vertical supports.
   - Incorporate flowers around the base and hanging from the canopy.

8. **Finishing Touches**
   - Add whimsical elements (small fairy figurines, crystals) throughout the structure.
   - Ensure stability and safety of the scaffold.

9. **Setup and Dismantle**
   - Assemble in a designated area with a flat surface.
   - Disassemble carefully, keeping materials for future use.

10. **Timeline**
    - Planning: 1 week
    - Assembly: 1 day
    - Dismantle: 1 day

**End of Plan**
