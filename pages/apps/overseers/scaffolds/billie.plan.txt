**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, Curious, Kind-hearted

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with flower motifs

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Role: Guardian of nature and harmony
   - Journey: Seeks to restore balance in her realm

4. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Primary Goal: Protect the Avalon forest from dark forces
   - Motivation: Love for nature and its creatures

5. **Abilities and Powers**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Flight: Swift and agile in the air
   - Healing Touch: Can heal minor wounds

6. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Antagonists: Dark spirits threatening Avalon

7. **Key Plot Points**
   - Discovery of a dark force in Avalon
   - Gathering allies to confront the threat
   - Personal growth through challenges faced

8. **Themes**
   - Friendship and teamwork
   - The importance of nature
   - Courage in the face of adversity

9. **Conclusion**
   - Resolution of conflict
   - Restoration of peace in Avalon
   - Billie’s growth as a leader and protector
