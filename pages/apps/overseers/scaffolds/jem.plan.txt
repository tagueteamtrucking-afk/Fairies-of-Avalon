**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Jem'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Jem
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Avalon
   - Personality Traits: Playful, curious, kind-hearted

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Size: Small, typical fairy stature
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress, adorned with flowers and leaves

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted forest of Avalon
   - Journey: Discovered her powers at a young age
   - Purpose: To protect the magical realm and assist lost travelers

4. **Powers and Abilities**
   - Flight: Capable of swift and agile movement
   - Nature Manipulation: Can communicate with plants and animals
   - Healing: Ability to mend wounds and cure ailments

5. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Primary Goal: To maintain harmony in Avalon
   - Personal Motivation: Desire to explore the world beyond Avalon

6. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: An elder fairy who guides her
   - Conflict: Rivalry with a dark fairy seeking power

7. **Challenges and Conflicts**
   - Internal Conflict: Struggles with her curiosity leading her into danger
   - External Conflict: Protecting Avalon from dark forces

8. **Character Arc**
   - Growth: Learns the importance of balance between curiosity and responsibility
   - Resolution: Embraces her role as a guardian while exploring her dreams

9. **Setting**
   - Primary Location: Enchanted forest of Avalon
   - Key Locations: Crystal clear pond, ancient oak tree, hidden glade

10. **Themes**
    - Friendship: The importance of bonds with others
    - Adventure: The thrill of exploration and discovery
    - Responsibility: Balancing personal desires with duties to others

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the character of Jem within the Avalon Fairy narrative.
