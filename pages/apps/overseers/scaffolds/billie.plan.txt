**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Floral dress, adorned with natural elements

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Cheerful and optimistic
   - Loves to explore and discover new things
   - Protective of her friends and the environment

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (encouraging plant growth)
   - Flight with agility and grace
   - Communication with woodland creatures

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Mission: To maintain harmony in nature and assist lost creatures

6. **Goals**
   - To protect her home from dark forces
   - To foster friendships with other magical beings

7. **Challenges**
   - Overcoming her fears of the unknown
   - Balancing her playful nature with responsibilities

8. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Rivals: Dark spirits threatening Avalon

9. **Setting**
   - Enchanted Avalon forest, filled with vibrant flora and fauna

10. **Plot Hooks**
    - Discovering a hidden treasure that could save Avalon
    - Encountering a mysterious creature that needs help

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Billie'.
