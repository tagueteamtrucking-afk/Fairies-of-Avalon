**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings for stability

2. **Main Structure**
   - Frame: Wooden beams and columns
   - Walls: Lightweight, weather-resistant panels

3. **Roof**
   - A-frame design for rain runoff
   - Shingle or thatch roofing material

4. **Windows & Doors**
   - Arched windows for fairy aesthetic
   - Wooden door with decorative carvings

5. **Interior Layout**
   - Open floor plan for communal space
   - Designated areas for sleeping, crafting, and gathering

6. **Exterior Features**
   - Garden space for herbs and flowers
   - Pathways made of natural stone

7. **Utilities**
   - Basic plumbing for water access
   - Solar panels for sustainable energy

8. **Finishing Touches**
   - Fairy lights for ambiance
   - Natural wood finishes for a whimsical look

9. **Landscaping**
   - Surrounding flora to blend with the environment
   - Seating areas with natural materials

10. **Safety Measures**
    - Fire safety protocols
    - Structural integrity checks

This scaffold plan provides a foundational outline for constructing the Avalon Fairy 'Abbey' while maintaining a minimalistic approach.
