**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base materials (wooden beams or concrete blocks)

2. **Structural Framework**
   - Vertical supports (4 corner posts)
   - Horizontal beams (top and mid-level)

3. **Walls**
   - Lightweight panels (wood or fabric)
   - Openings for windows and doors

4. **Roof**
   - A-frame or gabled structure
   - Lightweight roofing material (thatch or shingles)

5. **Access**
   - Ladder or stairs for entry
   - Safety railings (if applicable)

6. **Finishing Touches**
   - Decorative elements (fairy lights, flowers)
   - Weatherproofing (sealants or coatings)

7. **Safety Measures**
   - Stability checks
   - Weight distribution considerations

8. **Inspection**
   - Ensure structural integrity
   - Compliance with safety standards

This scaffold plan serves as a basic outline for constructing the Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'.
