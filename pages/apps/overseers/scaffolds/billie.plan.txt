**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and guide for lost creatures

3. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling flower petals
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Light, ethereal dress made of leaves and flowers

4. **Personality Traits**
   - Positive: Kind-hearted, adventurous, optimistic
   - Flaws: Impulsive, sometimes naive

5. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Healing: Uses magic to heal minor injuries

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect the Avalon forest and its inhabitants
   - Explore beyond her home and learn about other realms

7. **Conflict**
   - External: Threat from a dark force seeking to corrupt Avalon
   - Internal: Struggles with self-doubt about her abilities

8. **Character Arc**
   - Growth: Learns to trust her instincts and embrace her powers
   - Resolution: Defeats the dark force and solidifies her role as a protector

9. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who guides Billie
   - Friend: A mischievous woodland creature who provides comic relief

10. **Setting**
    - Avalon Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical creatures and hidden secrets

11. **Themes**
    - Friendship: The importance of companionship and teamwork
    - Courage: Facing fears and standing up for what is right

12. **Conclusion**
    - Billie emerges as a stronger guardian, ready to face new adventures.
