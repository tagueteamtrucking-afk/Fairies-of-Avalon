**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Avalon Forest

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight
   - Communication with animals

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain balance in the forest

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the Avalon Forest from threats
   - Foster harmony between creatures and nature

7. **Challenges**
   - Encroachment of human development
   - Conflicts with other magical beings

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest creatures (e.g., deer, birds)

9. **Conflict Resolution**
   - Use of diplomacy and magic
   - Seeking guidance from elder fairies

10. **Conclusion**
    - Billie’s journey revolves around growth, protection, and the celebration of nature.
