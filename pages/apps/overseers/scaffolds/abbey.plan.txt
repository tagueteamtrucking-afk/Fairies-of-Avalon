**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings for stability

2. **Structure**
   - Wooden frame (2x4s) for walls
   - Roof trusses for overhead support

3. **Walls**
   - Exterior: Plywood sheathing
   - Interior: Gypsum board for insulation

4. **Roof**
   - Asphalt shingles or thatch for fairy aesthetic
   - Gutter system for rainwater management

5. **Access**
   - Sturdy ladder or staircase for entry
   - Safety railings around elevated areas

6. **Utilities (if applicable)**
   - Basic electrical wiring for lighting
   - Plumbing for water features (optional)

7. **Finishing Touches**
   - Paint or natural finishes for aesthetics
   - Decorative elements (e.g., fairy lights, floral designs)

8. **Safety Measures**
   - Regular inspections of structural integrity
   - Clear pathways and emergency exits

**Note**: Adjust dimensions and materials based on specific design requirements and local building codes.
