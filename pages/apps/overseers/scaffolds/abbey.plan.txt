**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Main Structure**
   - Frame construction (wood or metal)
   - Wall panels (insulated)

3. **Roof**
   - Truss system
   - Roof sheathing
   - Waterproof membrane

4. **Windows and Doors**
   - Pre-framed openings
   - Installation of energy-efficient windows
   - Door frames and hardware

5. **Interior Layout**
   - Basic partition walls
   - Electrical wiring and plumbing rough-ins

6. **Exterior Finishes**
   - Siding (wood, vinyl, or stone)
   - Trim and fascia

7. **Landscaping**
   - Grading and drainage
   - Planting beds and pathways

8. **Utilities**
   - Connection to water, electricity, and sewage systems

9. **Safety Features**
   - Fire exits
   - Handrails for stairways

10. **Final Touches**
    - Interior finishes (drywall, paint, flooring)
    - Exterior landscaping and lighting

**Note:** Adjustments may be needed based on local building codes and site conditions.
