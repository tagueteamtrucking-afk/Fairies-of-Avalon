**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - To create a scaffold plan for the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' project.

2. **Project Overview**
   - Description: Development of a fairy-themed character named Alexandria.
   - Target Audience: Children aged 5-10.

3. **Key Components**
   - Character Design
     - Visual appearance (wings, attire, colors)
     - Personality traits (kind, adventurous, wise)
   - Storyline
     - Background (origin, magical abilities)
     - Key adventures and challenges
   - Merchandise
     - Toys (figurines, plushies)
     - Books (storybooks, activity books)
     - Apparel (clothing, accessories)

4. **Timeline**
   - Research & Concept Development: Month 1
   - Character Design Finalization: Month 2
   - Storyline Development: Month 3
   - Prototype Creation: Month 4
   - Marketing Strategy: Month 5
   - Launch: Month 6

5. **Budget Overview**
   - Design Costs
   - Production Costs
   - Marketing Costs
   - Distribution Costs

6. **Team Roles**
   - Project Manager: Oversees the project timeline and budget.
   - Designer: Responsible for character and merchandise design.
   - Writer: Develops the storyline and accompanying literature.
   - Marketing Specialist: Plans promotional strategies.

7. **Evaluation Metrics**
   - Audience engagement (feedback, surveys)
   - Sales figures (initial launch and ongoing)
   - Brand recognition (social media presence, partnerships)

8. **Next Steps**
   - Finalize team assignments.
   - Begin research on target audience preferences.
   - Schedule initial brainstorming sessions.
