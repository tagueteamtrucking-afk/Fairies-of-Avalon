**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Carol'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Carol
   - Role: Fairy of Winter
   - Personality Traits: Cheerful, nurturing, playful

2. **Physical Description**
   - Appearance: Delicate features, shimmering ice-blue wings, silver hair
   - Attire: Frost-themed gown, adorned with snowflake patterns

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the Winter Realm of Avalon
   - Journey: Discovered her powers while helping lost creatures in the snow

4. **Abilities**
   - Ice Manipulation: Can create snowflakes and frost patterns
   - Healing Touch: Uses winter herbs to heal others
   - Flight: Swift and graceful movement through the air

5. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Primary Goal: To spread joy during the winter season
   - Motivation: Belief in the magic of kindness and community

6. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other seasonal fairies, woodland creatures
   - Conflicts: Rivalry with the Summer Fairy over seasonal dominance

7. **Key Plot Points**
   - Introduction: Carol’s arrival in Avalon during the first snowfall
   - Conflict: A winter storm threatens the balance of the seasons
   - Resolution: Carol unites the fairies to restore harmony

8. **Themes**
   - Friendship: The importance of collaboration among different beings
   - Change: Embracing the beauty of seasonal transitions

9. **Visual Elements**
   - Color Palette: Ice blue, silver, white
   - Symbolism: Snowflakes representing individuality and unity

10. **Conclusion**
    - Carol’s legacy: A reminder of the beauty in winter and the warmth of community.
