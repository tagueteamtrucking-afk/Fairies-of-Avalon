**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Create a framework for the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' project.

2. **Project Overview**
   - Concept: A magical fairy character named Alexandria.
   - Theme: Enchantment, nature, and adventure.

3. **Key Elements**
   - Character Design
     - Appearance: Ethereal features, nature-inspired attire.
     - Personality: Curious, wise, and playful.
   - Backstory
     - Origin: Born from a mystical forest.
     - Purpose: Guardian of nature and guide for lost souls.

4. **Setting**
   - Environment: Enchanted forest with vibrant flora and fauna.
   - Key Locations: Fairy glen, crystal pond, ancient tree.

5. **Plot Outline**
   - Introduction: Alexandria discovers a disturbance in her forest.
   - Conflict: A dark force threatens the balance of nature.
   - Resolution: Alexandria embarks on a quest to restore harmony.

6. **Target Audience**
   - Age Group: Children and young adults.
   - Themes: Friendship, courage, and environmental stewardship.

7. **Visual Style**
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with vibrant highlights.
   - Art Style: Whimsical and illustrative.

8. **Development Timeline**
   - Phase 1: Concept Art and Character Design (Month 1-2)
   - Phase 2: Story Development and Scriptwriting (Month 3-4)
   - Phase 3: Prototype Creation (Month 5-6)

9. **Budget Overview**
   - Estimated Costs: Character design, marketing, production.
   - Funding Sources: Crowdfunding, sponsorships, merchandise.

10. **Evaluation Metrics**
    - Audience Engagement: Feedback and surveys.
    - Project Milestones: Adherence to timeline and budget.
