**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Structure**
   - Main frame: Timber beams (2x4)
   - Vertical supports: Spaced 4 feet apart
   - Roof trusses: Pre-fabricated or built on-site

3. **Walls**
   - Exterior: Plywood sheathing
   - Insulation: Fiberglass batts
   - Interior: Gypsum board (drywall)

4. **Roof**
   - Roofing material: Asphalt shingles or metal
   - Overhang: 1-foot eaves

5. **Windows and Doors**
   - Standard size windows: Double-glazed
   - Main entry door: Solid wood or fiberglass

6. **Finishing**
   - Exterior paint/stain
   - Interior paint: Non-toxic, low-VOC

7. **Utilities**
   - Electrical: Basic wiring for lighting and outlets
   - Plumbing: Simple fixtures (sink, toilet)

8. **Landscaping**
   - Minimal planting: Native plants around the perimeter
   - Pathways: Gravel or stepping stones

9. **Safety Features**
   - Handrails on stairs
   - Smoke detectors installed

10. **Compliance**
    - Ensure adherence to local building codes and regulations.
