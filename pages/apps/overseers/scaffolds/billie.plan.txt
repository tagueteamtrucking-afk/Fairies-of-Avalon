**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, mischievous

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with floral patterns

3. **Setting**
   - Location: Enchanted forest of Avalon
   - Environment: Lush greenery, sparkling streams, magical flora

4. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from a blooming flower in Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of nature and mischief-maker among forest creatures

5. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect the forest and its inhabitants
   - Explore new areas and discover hidden secrets

6. **Challenges**
   - Balancing playful nature with responsibilities
   - Facing threats from dark forces seeking to disrupt Avalon

7. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Antagonists: Dark spirits, humans who harm nature

8. **Character Arc**
   - Growth from playful mischief to understanding the weight of responsibility
   - Learning to harness her powers for the greater good

9. **Key Themes**
   - Nature conservation
   - The balance between fun and duty
   - Friendship and community

10. **Conclusion**
    - Billie’s journey emphasizes the importance of protecting the magic of nature while embracing one’s true self.
