**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Themis'**

1. **Objective**: Create a scaffold for the Avalon Fairy 'Themis' character, focusing on key attributes and storyline integration.

2. **Character Overview**:
   - Name: Themis
   - Role: Guardian of Justice and Balance
   - Appearance: Ethereal, with shimmering wings and a gown that reflects the colors of the dawn.

3. **Key Attributes**:
   - Wisdom: Deep knowledge of laws and fairness.
   - Empathy: Ability to understand and feel the emotions of others.
   - Strength: Fierce protector of the innocent and the balance of nature.

4. **Backstory**:
   - Origin: Born from the first light of dawn, Themis embodies the principles of justice.
   - Journey: Tasked with maintaining harmony in Avalon, she faces challenges from dark forces seeking chaos.

5. **Powers and Abilities**:
   - Light Manipulation: Can create barriers of light to protect or restrain.
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal wounds and soothe emotional pain.
   - Judgment: Can reveal truths and expose lies, ensuring fairness.

6. **Allies**:
   - Avalon Council: A group of fairies who assist in maintaining order.
   - Nature Spirits: Creatures of the forest that provide guidance and support.

7. **Antagonists**:
   - Dark Sorceress: Seeks to disrupt the balance and unleash chaos in Avalon.
   - Corrupted Fairies: Once allies, now turned against Themis due to envy and greed.

8. **Plot Points**:
   - Discovery of a dark plot threatening Avalon.
   - Themis’s internal struggle with doubt and fear.
   - The final confrontation with the dark sorceress, leading to a resolution that restores balance.

9. **Themes**:
   - Justice vs. Injustice
   - The importance of balance in nature and society.
   - The power of empathy and understanding.

10. **Conclusion**:
   - Themis emerges victorious, reinforcing the values of justice and harmony in Avalon, setting the stage for future adventures.
