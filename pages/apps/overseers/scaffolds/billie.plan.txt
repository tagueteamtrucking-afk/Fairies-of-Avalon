**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Avalon

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 6 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with flower motifs

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of her realm

4. **Abilities**
   - Flight
   - Nature manipulation (growth of plants)
   - Healing touch

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Journey: Tasked with protecting the balance of nature

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Help lost creatures find their way home

7. **Challenges**
   - Encounters with dark forces threatening Avalon
   - Struggles with self-doubt in her abilities

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy who guides her
   - Friend: A mischievous woodland creature

9. **Setting**
   - Location: Enchanted forest of Avalon
   - Key Locations: Crystal-clear pond, ancient oak tree, flower meadow

10. **Plot Points**
    - Discovery of a dark force
    - Quest to gather allies
    - Final confrontation to save Avalon

11. **Themes**
    - Friendship
    - Courage
    - The importance of nature

12. **Conclusion**
    - Restoration of Avalon
    - Billie’s growth as a guardian and leader
