**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective:**
   - Develop a fairy character named Alexandria with unique attributes and storyline.

2. **Character Profile:**
   - Name: Alexandria
   - Species: Fairy
   - Element: Air
   - Personality Traits: Curious, Kind, Adventurous
   - Appearance: Ethereal wings, flowing hair, pastel-colored attire

3. **Backstory:**
   - Origin: Born in the Whispering Woods, Alexandria has always been fascinated by the human world.
   - Motivation: Seeks to bridge the gap between fairies and humans, fostering understanding and friendship.

4. **Abilities:**
   - Flight: Can soar through the skies with grace.
   - Wind Manipulation: Controls gentle breezes and strong gusts.
   - Healing Touch: Can heal small wounds with her magic.

5. **Setting:**
   - Primary Location: Whispering Woods, a mystical forest filled with magical creatures and hidden secrets.
   - Key Locations: 
     - The Crystal Glade (meeting place for fairies)
     - The Human Village (where Alexandria interacts with humans)

6. **Plot Points:**
   - Introduction: Alexandria discovers a human in distress and decides to help.
   - Conflict: Faces challenges from both the fairy council and human skeptics.
   - Resolution: Unites fairies and humans to solve a common problem, showcasing the power of collaboration.

7. **Supporting Characters:**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy Luna, wise and nurturing.
   - Friend: A human child named Leo, adventurous and open-minded.
   - Antagonist: A skeptical fairy named Thorn, resistant to change.

8. **Themes:**
   - Friendship: The bond between different species.
   - Courage: Standing up for what is right.
   - Understanding: Bridging cultural divides.

9. **Visual Elements:**
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with hints of gold.
   - Art Style: Whimsical and enchanting, emphasizing nature and magic.

10. **Conclusion:**
    - Alexandria’s journey emphasizes the importance of empathy and cooperation, leaving a lasting impact on both fairies and humans.
