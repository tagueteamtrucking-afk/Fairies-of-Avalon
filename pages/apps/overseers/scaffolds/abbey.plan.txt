**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base layer of compacted gravel

2. **Main Structure**
   - Vertical posts (4) at corners
   - Horizontal beams (2) for top support
   - Cross-bracing for stability

3. **Walls**
   - Lightweight panels (wood or fabric) for enclosure
   - Openings for windows and doors

4. **Roof**
   - Sloped design for rain runoff
   - Lightweight roofing material (thatch or fabric)

5. **Access**
   - Ladder or stairs for entry
   - Safety railing around elevated areas

6. **Finishing Touches**
   - Decorative elements (fairy lights, flowers)
   - Natural camouflage (moss, branches)

7. **Safety Checks**
   - Stability assessment
   - Weight distribution evaluation

8. **Maintenance Plan**
   - Regular inspections for wear
   - Seasonal adjustments for weather conditions

**Note:** Ensure all materials are eco-friendly and suitable for a fairy habitat.
