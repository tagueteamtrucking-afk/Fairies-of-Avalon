**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with floral patterns

3. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal minor injuries and ailments
   - Illusion Creation: Can create visual illusions to protect or entertain

4. **Personality**
   - Friendly and approachable
   - Enjoys exploring new environments
   - Values friendship and harmony in nature

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Journey: Has traveled to various realms to learn about different cultures and nature

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the natural world
   - Foster connections between fairies and other creatures
   - Discover new magical abilities

7. **Challenges**
   - Balancing her playful nature with responsibilities
   - Overcoming obstacles posed by dark forces threatening Avalon

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who guides Billie
   - Friends: A group of diverse magical creatures

9. **Setting**
   - Primary Location: Enchanted Avalon forest
   - Key Locations: Magical glades, hidden waterfalls, ancient trees

10. **Plot Points**
    - Initial discovery of her powers
    - Encounter with a dark force
    - Journey to unite different magical beings against a common threat

11. **Conclusion**
    - Resolution of conflicts
    - Growth and maturity of Billie as a fairy
    - Establishment of a new harmony in Avalon
