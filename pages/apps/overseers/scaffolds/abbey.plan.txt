**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base support structure

2. **Main Structure**
   - Vertical supports (4 main posts)
   - Horizontal beams (top and bottom)

3. **Walls**
   - Lightweight panels (wood or fabric)
   - Openings for windows and doors

4. **Roof**
   - Sloped design for rain runoff
   - Lightweight roofing material (thatch or fabric)

5. **Access**
   - Ladder or stairs for entry
   - Safety railings if necessary

6. **Finishing Touches**
   - Decorative elements (fairy lights, flowers)
   - Paint or stain for aesthetics

7. **Safety Checks**
   - Stability assessment
   - Weight distribution evaluation

8. **Maintenance Plan**
   - Regular inspections
   - Repairs as needed

**Note:** Ensure all materials are eco-friendly and suitable for outdoor use.
