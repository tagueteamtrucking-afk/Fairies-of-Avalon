**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Avalon

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 6 inches
   - Wing Type: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Flight
   - Nature manipulation (e.g., encouraging plant growth)
   - Healing touch

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain balance in the magical ecosystem

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect Avalon from dark forces
   - Foster harmony between magical creatures and nature

7. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Rivals: Dark sorcerers, pollution entities

8. **Conflict**
   - Main Antagonist: A dark sorcerer threatening Avalon
   - Internal Conflict: Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

9. **Development Arc**
   - Growth from playful guardian to a wise protector
   - Learning the importance of sacrifice for the greater good

10. **Conclusion**
    - Resolution of conflict through teamwork and courage
    - Establishment of a new era of peace in Avalon
