**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Tracy'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Tracy
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels (lavender, mint green, pale pink)
   - Distinct Features: Flower crown, shimmering skin

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of her realm

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Illusion Creation: Can create visual illusions to protect her territory

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Mission: To maintain balance in nature and protect the magical realm

6. **Allies and Enemies**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Enemies: Dark spirits, pollution

7. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Primary Goal: Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Motivation: Love for nature and its creatures

8. **Challenges**
   - Threats from industrialization
   - Maintaining harmony among different fairy factions

9. **Conclusion**
   - Tracy embodies the spirit of nature and serves as a beacon of hope in Avalon.
