**Avalon Fairy 'Abbey' Scaffold Plan**

1. **Foundation**
   - Size: 10m x 10m
   - Material: Reinforced concrete
   - Depth: 1m

2. **Vertical Supports**
   - Type: Steel columns
   - Spacing: Every 2m
   - Height: 4m

3. **Horizontal Beams**
   - Type: Steel I-beams
   - Placement: Top of vertical supports
   - Size: 10m length

4. **Decking**
   - Material: Plywood sheets
   - Thickness: 18mm
   - Coverage: Entire scaffold area

5. **Safety Features**
   - Guardrails: Installed at 1m height around the perimeter
   - Toe boards: 150mm high at the base of guardrails
   - Access ladder: 3m height, secured to the scaffold

6. **Stabilization**
   - Diagonal bracing: Installed between vertical supports
   - Base plates: 300mm x 300mm under each vertical support

7. **Inspection**
   - Frequency: Daily before use
   - Checklist: Structural integrity, safety features, and stability

8. **Dismantling Plan**
   - Sequence: Remove guardrails, then decking, followed by vertical supports
   - Safety: Ensure area is clear of personnel before dismantling

**End of Scaffold Plan**
