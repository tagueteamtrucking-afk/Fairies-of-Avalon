**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Odessa'**

1. **Objective**: Create a lightweight scaffold to support the design and construction of the Avalon Fairy 'Odessa'.

2. **Materials**:
   - Lightweight aluminum or steel tubes
   - Connectors and joints
   - Safety harnesses and guardrails
   - Non-slip platforming

3. **Dimensions**:
   - Height: 10 feet
   - Width: 6 feet
   - Depth: 4 feet

4. **Structure**:
   - Base: Four corner posts with cross-bracing for stability.
   - Platform: A single level platform at 8 feet height.
   - Access: Ladder or stairway on one side for safe entry and exit.

5. **Safety Features**:
   - Guardrails around the platform.
   - Non-slip surface on the platform.
   - Regular inspections for structural integrity.

6. **Assembly Instructions**:
   - Assemble base frame first, ensuring all joints are secure.
   - Attach vertical posts to each corner of the base.
   - Install cross-bracing between posts for added stability.
   - Secure platform to the top of the posts.
   - Install access ladder/stairway.

7. **Disassembly Instructions**:
   - Remove the access ladder/stairway first.
   - Detach the platform from the posts.
   - Disassemble the vertical posts and cross-bracing.
   - Take apart the base frame last.

8. **Maintenance**:
   - Inspect for rust or wear every month.
   - Tighten any loose connections immediately.

9. **Storage**:
   - Store in a dry, secure location when not in use.

10. **Compliance**:
    - Ensure all materials and construction meet local safety regulations.
