**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and protector of magical creatures

3. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and heal animals
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Illusion Creation: Can create visual illusions to protect her realm

5. **Personality Traits**
   - Friendly: Welcomes all creatures
   - Mischievous: Enjoys playful pranks on friends
   - Wise: Offers guidance to those in need

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect Avalon: Maintain the balance of nature
   - Explore: Discover new lands and creatures
   - Foster Friendship: Build connections with other fairies and beings

7. **Challenges**
   - Dark Forces: Threats from those who wish to exploit Avalon
   - Self-Doubt: Struggles with her own abilities and purpose

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who guides Billie
   - Best Friend: A small woodland creature who accompanies her
   - Rival: A mischievous sprite who challenges her

9. **Setting**
   - Avalon Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical flora and fauna
   - Hidden Glades: Secret areas where Billie can recharge her powers

10. **Plot Points**
    - Discovery of a dark force threatening Avalon
    - Journey to find allies and gather strength
    - Confrontation with the antagonist and resolution of the conflict

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the character of Billie, the Avalon Fairy.
