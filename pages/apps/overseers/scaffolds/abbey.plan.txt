**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Structure**
   - Main frame: Wooden beams (2x4)
   - Vertical supports: Spaced every 4 feet
   - Cross bracing for stability

3. **Walls**
   - Exterior: Plywood sheathing
   - Interior: Insulation and drywall

4. **Roof**
   - Trusses: Pre-fabricated or built on-site
   - Roofing material: Shingles or thatch

5. **Access**
   - Stairs: Wooden steps
   - Railing: Safety railings around elevated areas

6. **Utilities**
   - Electrical: Basic wiring plan
   - Plumbing: Simple water lines for sinks

7. **Finishing**
   - Exterior paint or stain
   - Interior fixtures: Lighting, cabinetry

8. **Landscaping**
   - Surrounding area: Grass, flower beds
   - Pathways: Stone or gravel paths leading to the entrance

**Notes:**
- Ensure compliance with local building codes.
- Consider environmental impact and sustainability.
