**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Structure**
   - Wooden frame (2x4s for walls)
   - Roof trusses (gable design)
   - Exterior sheathing (plywood)

3. **Walls**
   - Install wall frames
   - Insulation (fiberglass or foam)
   - Exterior siding (wood or composite)

4. **Roof**
   - Install roofing material (shingles or metal)
   - Ventilation (ridge vents)

5. **Windows and Doors**
   - Frame openings for windows and doors
   - Install energy-efficient windows
   - Exterior door installation

6. **Interior**
   - Drywall installation
   - Electrical wiring and outlets
   - Plumbing rough-in (if applicable)

7. **Finishing**
   - Interior painting
   - Flooring installation (wood, tile, or carpet)
   - Trim and molding

8. **Exterior**
   - Landscaping (plants, pathways)
   - Deck or patio installation (if applicable)

9. **Utilities**
   - Connect to water, electricity, and sewage
   - Install heating/cooling systems

10. **Final Inspection**
    - Ensure compliance with local building codes
    - Final walkthrough for quality assurance

**Note:** Adjust materials and dimensions based on specific design requirements and local regulations.
