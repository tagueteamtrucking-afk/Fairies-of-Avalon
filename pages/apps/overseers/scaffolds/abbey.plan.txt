**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Concrete footings

2. **Main Structure**
   - Frame: Wooden beams (2x4 or 2x6)
   - Walls: Plywood sheathing
   - Roof: Truss system with plywood decking

3. **Exterior**
   - Siding: Wood or composite panels
   - Windows: Energy-efficient glass
   - Doors: Solid wood or fiberglass

4. **Interior Layout**
   - Rooms: Designate spaces for living, sleeping, and bathing
   - Hallways: Ensure accessibility and flow

5. **Utilities**
   - Electrical: Basic wiring plan
   - Plumbing: Water supply and drainage layout
   - HVAC: Heating and cooling system design

6. **Finishing Touches**
   - Insulation: Walls and roof
   - Drywall: Interior wall covering
   - Flooring: Wood, tile, or carpet options

7. **Landscaping**
   - Garden beds: Native plants and flowers
   - Pathways: Stone or gravel paths

8. **Safety Features**
   - Smoke detectors: Install in key areas
   - Fire extinguishers: Accessible locations

9. **Sustainability**
   - Rainwater collection: Gutters and storage
   - Solar panels: Roof-mounted system

10. **Regulatory Compliance**
    - Building permits: Obtain necessary approvals
    - Zoning laws: Adhere to local regulations

**Note:** This scaffold plan is a basic outline and should be tailored to specific design requirements and local building codes.
