# Fairies of Avalon – Character Overview

This summary consolidates character information from the repository’s memory files, planning documents and prior discussions to provide a clear, unified view of each fairy.  It reflects the latest assignments (e.g., Charlotte absorbing Nina’s duties) and the intended scope of their micro‑apps.

## Alexandria

* **Role:** Storyteller and lore keeper.  Alexandria lives in Avalon but crafts stories set in other realms, collaborating with the user to build a multiverse.  She curates the story shelf and maintains character sheets, and she will eventually host a timeline explorer.  An interactive “story assistant” allows conversational brainstorming.
* **Interactions:** Works closely with Tracy (art) and Stella (audio) to turn stories into comics, animations and games.  Needs the ability to understand, categorise and suggest ideas, possibly via safe web access.

## Tracy

* **Role:** Artist and skin designer.  Tracy produces 2D and 3D art including skins, posters and illustrations.  She draws characters created with Alexandria and ensures continuity and consistency across images.  Her tools include an art gallery, skin designer, poster creator and story art workspace.
* **Future:** Will integrate an AI image generator that honours every visual detail, enabling creation of manga‑style comics and anime with Alexandria’s scripts and Stella’s audio.  Eventually may contribute assets for mobile games.

## Charlotte (formerly Charlotte + Nina + Themis)

* **Role:** Translation relay, compliance manager and technical fabricator.  Charlotte now encompasses all of Nina’s former responsibilities, including infrastructure and 3D rendering.  She manages queues, translations, expirations, reminders and a VRM/scene viewer.  New pages include an armor builder and a 3D workshop where users can configure armour, weapons and accessories.
* **Note on Nina:** Nina remains in memory as the original engineering and security overseer, but all her tasks (security reports, import map validation, VRM viewer, scene index) have been reassigned to Charlotte.

## Billie (Billie + Odessa + Sorcha)

* **Role:** Monetisation and social media overseer.  Billie handles social posts, an earnings dashboard and a grants tracker.  Her remit now expands to include a video maker capable of generating faceless demos and deepfake‑style videos once connected to appropriate services.  She will also explore other ultra‑realistic video tools.

## Carol

* **Role:** Nutrition assistant.  Carol manages meal plans, shopping lists and nutrition reports.  The meal planner now loads a two‑week plan from a JSON file and lets users rate each meal (1–5) and record substitutions or notes; feedback is stored in local storage so it doesn’t alter the original plan.  These ratings will inform future meal recommendations.  The shopping list compiles ingredients, and the menu page showcases culinary creations.

## Jem

* **Role:** Fitness assistant.  Jem manages workout plans, a workout calendar and a progress tracker.  Week 1 of the training program has been redesigned as an **Evaluation and Warm‑Up** week: users set a body‑style goal (lean, athletic, muscular, etc.), record baseline metrics (weight, measurements) and perform light cardio and bodyweight tests to gauge current fitness.  Subsequent days assess upper‑body, lower‑body and cardiovascular endurance with simple exercises.  The week concludes with a summary to prepare for structured body‑sculpting in later weeks.
* **Goal Selection:** Jem provides a selection of body styles to choose from as an end goal and uses the baseline assessments to tailor a plan that progresses safely toward that goal.

## Stella

* **Role:** Voice and sound assistant.  Stella offers text‑to‑speech playback, a sound library and guided sessions.  She is being enhanced with the ability to write her own meditation scripts, using natural language techniques, gateway program sounds and hypnotic structures.  Users can save favourite scripts, manage a script library and delete unwanted files.

## Overseers (Rey Czar & White Star)

* **Role:** Governance and infrastructure.  The Overseers manage the palace hub, permissions registry and telemetry dashboard.  They act as administrators ensuring the city runs smoothly.

## Legacy Fairies

* **Nina:** Originally the engineering and security overseer, responsible for infrastructure and 3D rendering.  Her functions (security reports, import map validation, VRM viewer, scene index) have been fully absorbed by Charlotte.  Nina remains in the memory history for historical reference.
