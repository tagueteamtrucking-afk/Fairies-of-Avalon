**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Charlotte'**

1. **Objective:**
   - Create a scaffold for the character Charlotte, focusing on her traits, background, and role in the Avalon Fairy narrative.

2. **Character Overview:**
   - Name: Charlotte
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

3. **Physical Description:**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leafy dress adorned with flowers

4. **Personality Traits:**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Curious
   - Playful
   - Protective of her realm

5. **Background:**
   - Origin: Born in the Enchanted Forest of Avalon
   - Family: Comes from a lineage of nature guardians
   - Special Abilities: Communicates with animals, controls plant growth

6. **Goals and Motivations:**
   - Protect the natural balance of Avalon
   - Help lost creatures find their way home
   - Discover the secrets of the ancient forest

7. **Challenges:**
   - Encounters with dark forces threatening Avalon
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities
   - Learning to harness her powers effectively

8. **Relationships:**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: An elder fairy who guides her
   - Antagonist: A dark sorceress seeking to corrupt Avalon

9. **Key Story Arcs:**
   - Journey to restore a dying part of the forest
   - Overcoming self-doubt and embracing her powers
   - Forming alliances with other magical beings

10. **Conclusion:**
    - Charlotte's growth as a character and her impact on Avalon’s future.
