**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**: Define the purpose and goals of the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' project.

2. **Target Audience**: Identify the primary audience for the project (e.g., children, families, fantasy enthusiasts).

3. **Key Features**:
   - Character Design: Develop the visual and personality traits of Alexandria.
   - Storyline: Outline the main plot and adventures of Alexandria.
   - Environment: Create the setting where Alexandria interacts (e.g., enchanted forest, magical kingdom).

4. **Content Creation**:
   - Illustrations: Plan for artwork depicting Alexandria and her world.
   - Narratives: Write short stories or descriptions of Alexandria's adventures.
   - Interactive Elements: Consider games or activities related to Alexandria's character.

5. **Marketing Strategy**:
   - Branding: Develop a logo and visual identity for Alexandria.
   - Promotion: Outline channels for reaching the target audience (social media, events, etc.).

6. **Timeline**:
   - Milestones: Set key dates for completion of character design, story development, and marketing launch.

7. **Budget**:
   - Estimate costs for artwork, marketing, and distribution.

8. **Evaluation**:
   - Metrics: Determine how success will be measured (engagement, sales, feedback).

9. **Feedback Loop**:
   - Plan for gathering audience feedback to refine and enhance the project. 

10. **Next Steps**: Outline immediate actions to kick off the project.
