**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Avalon Grove

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leafy dress adorned with flowers

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (growth of plants)
   - Flight
   - Healing touch

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain balance in the magical ecosystem

6. **Goals**
   - Protect the Avalon Grove from dark forces
   - Foster harmony between fairies and nature

7. **Challenges**
   - Encounters with dark creatures
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest animals

9. **Conflict**
   - A looming threat to the Avalon Grove that tests her abilities and resolve

10. **Resolution**
    - Billie learns to harness her powers and unites her allies to restore peace.
