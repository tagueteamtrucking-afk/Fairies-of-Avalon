**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Project Overview**
   - Name: Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'
   - Objective: Create a whimsical and enchanting fairy character.

2. **Character Design**
   - Appearance: Ethereal features, flowing garments, delicate wings.
   - Color Palette: Pastels with hints of iridescence.
   - Accessories: Nature-inspired jewelry, magical staff.

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the essence of a mystical forest.
   - Role: Guardian of knowledge and wisdom in the fairy realm.

4. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Control over nature and the ability to communicate with animals.
   - Knowledge: Keeper of ancient fairy lore and secrets.

5. **Environment**
   - Habitat: Enchanted forest with luminescent flora and fauna.
   - Key Locations: Library of whispers, crystal-clear pond.

6. **Interactions**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures.
   - Enemies: Dark spirits, pollution threats.

7. **Goals**
   - Protect the forest and its secrets.
   - Educate other beings about the importance of nature.

8. **Visual References**
   - Gather images of ethereal landscapes, fairy costumes, and magical elements.

9. **Implementation Timeline**
   - Concept Art: 2 weeks
   - Character Model: 4 weeks
   - Environment Design: 3 weeks
   - Final Review: 1 week

10. **Feedback and Iteration**
    - Schedule regular check-ins for feedback on design and story elements. 

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' character.
