**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Purpose: Guardian of nature and harmony

3. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Outfit: Leaf and flower-inspired attire

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth
   - Healing Touch: Heals small creatures and plants
   - Flight: Swift and agile in the air

5. **Personality Traits**
   - Friendly and approachable
   - Adventurous spirit
   - Empathetic towards all living things

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect the Avalon forest
   - Foster friendships with other magical beings
   - Explore the world beyond Avalon

7. **Challenges**
   - Threats to the forest from outside forces
   - Balancing curiosity with responsibilities
   - Learning to trust others

8. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: An elder fairy who guides her
   - Rival: A mischievous sprite causing trouble

9. **Development Arc**
   - Growth from naive curiosity to wise protector
   - Learning the importance of teamwork and trust
   - Overcoming fears to confront challenges

10. **Conclusion**
    - Embrace her role as a guardian
    - Strengthen bonds with allies
    - Continue to explore and learn about the world
