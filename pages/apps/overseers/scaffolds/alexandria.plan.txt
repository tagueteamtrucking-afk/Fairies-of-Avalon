**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Define the purpose and goals of the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' project.

2. **Target Audience**
   - Identify the primary audience for the fairy character (e.g., children, fantasy enthusiasts).

3. **Character Design**
   - Outline key traits: 
     - Appearance (wings, attire, colors)
     - Personality (kind, adventurous, wise)
     - Background story (origin, magical abilities)

4. **Setting**
   - Describe the environment where Alexandria resides (e.g., enchanted forest, magical kingdom).

5. **Storyline**
   - Develop a basic plot arc:
     - Introduction (how Alexandria is introduced)
     - Conflict (challenges she faces)
     - Resolution (how she overcomes obstacles)

6. **Key Themes**
   - Identify central themes (e.g., friendship, courage, magic).

7. **Visual Elements**
   - Sketch ideas for illustrations or animations featuring Alexandria.

8. **Marketing Strategy**
   - Outline promotional methods (social media, merchandise, storytelling events).

9. **Feedback Mechanism**
   - Establish ways to gather audience feedback for future development.

10. **Timeline**
    - Create a timeline for project milestones and deadlines. 

This scaffold serves as a foundational framework for developing the Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' character and story.
