**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Jem'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Jem
   - Species: Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Enchanted Forest

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leafy dress adorned with flowers

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from a magical blossom in the heart of the forest
   - Purpose: To maintain harmony and protect the creatures of the forest

5. **Abilities**
   - Flight: Swift and agile
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Illusion: Creates visual illusions to protect her home

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - To safeguard the Enchanted Forest from external threats
   - To foster friendship among forest creatures

7. **Conflict**
   - Main Antagonist: A dark sorcerer seeking to drain the forest's magic
   - Internal Conflict: Balancing her playful nature with the seriousness of her responsibilities

8. **Key Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: An ancient tree spirit guiding her

9. **Setting**
   - Enchanted Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical flora and fauna

10. **Plot Points**
    - Discovery of the sorcerer's plan
    - Gathering allies for a confrontation
    - Final battle to protect the forest

11. **Resolution**
    - Restoration of balance in the forest
    - Jem's growth as a leader and protector

12. **Themes**
    - Friendship
    - Environmental stewardship
    - The balance of light and dark

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Jem within the Avalon Fairy narrative.
