**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base support structure (wooden beams or metal frames)

2. **Main Structure**
   - Vertical supports (4 corner posts)
   - Horizontal beams (top and mid-level)

3. **Walls**
   - Lightweight panels (wood or fabric)
   - Openings for windows and doors

4. **Roof**
   - Sloped design for rain runoff
   - Lightweight roofing material (thatch or shingles)

5. **Access**
   - Ladder or staircase for entry
   - Safety railings

6. **Decorative Elements**
   - Fairy lights
   - Natural elements (flowers, vines)

7. **Safety Measures**
   - Secure all joints and connections
   - Regular inspections for stability

8. **Finishing Touches**
   - Paint or stain for aesthetic appeal
   - Additional fairy-themed decorations

**Note:** Ensure compliance with local building codes and safety regulations.
