**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Curious
   - Playful
   - Wise

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (growth and healing)
   - Flight
   - Invisibility

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Purpose: To protect the balance of nature and assist lost creatures

6. **Goals**
   - Maintain harmony in Avalon
   - Help others find their way home
   - Discover the secrets of the ancient woods

7. **Challenges**
   - Dark forces threatening Avalon
   - Misunderstandings with other magical beings
   - Navigating her own fears of inadequacy

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest creatures
   - Ancient spirits of nature

9. **Conflict**
   - Confrontation with a dark sorceress
   - Internal struggle with self-doubt

10. **Resolution**
    - Overcoming fears through teamwork
    - Restoring balance to Avalon

11. **Themes**
    - Friendship
    - Courage
    - The importance of nature

12. **Visual Style**
    - Color Palette: Soft pastels with vibrant accents
    - Art Style: Whimsical and ethereal

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'.
