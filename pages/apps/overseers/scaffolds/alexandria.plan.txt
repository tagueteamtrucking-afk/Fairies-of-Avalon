**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective:**
   - Create a whimsical and enchanting environment for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' that reflects her personality and powers.

2. **Theme:**
   - Enchanted forest with elements of light, nature, and magic.

3. **Key Elements:**
   - **Color Palette:** Soft pastels (lavender, mint green, pale pink, sky blue).
   - **Materials:** Natural elements (wood, leaves, flowers), lightweight fabrics (tulle, silk), and shimmering accents (glitter, sequins).

4. **Structure:**
   - **Base:** Circular platform representing a fairy glade.
   - **Backdrop:** Flowing fabric drapes mimicking trees and foliage.
   - **Height Elements:** Tall, slender structures resembling tree trunks with fairy lights.

5. **Decorative Features:**
   - **Floral Arrangements:** Wildflowers and greenery scattered throughout.
   - **Lighting:** Soft, ambient lighting with fairy lights and lanterns.
   - **Interactive Elements:** Small areas for storytelling or interaction (e.g., a miniature wishing well).

6. **Functional Areas:**
   - **Performance Space:** Central area for fairy performances or storytelling.
   - **Resting Area:** Cozy nook with cushions for visitors to relax and engage with the fairy.

7. **Accessibility:**
   - Ensure pathways are clear and inviting for all visitors.

8. **Maintenance:**
   - Regular checks on lighting and natural elements to ensure a magical atmosphere is maintained.

9. **Timeline:**
   - **Week 1:** Design finalization and material sourcing.
   - **Week 2:** Construction of structures and installation of lighting.
   - **Week 3:** Decoration and final touches.

10. **Budget:**
    - Outline estimated costs for materials, labor, and maintenance.
