### Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'

#### 1. **Foundation**
   - **Materials**: Concrete footings, gravel base
   - **Dimensions**: 20 ft x 30 ft

#### 2. **Main Structure**
   - **Walls**: Wooden frame (2x4 studs)
   - **Height**: 12 ft at peak
   - **Roof**: Gable style, asphalt shingles

#### 3. **Flooring**
   - **Material**: Plywood subfloor
   - **Finish**: Hardwood or laminate

#### 4. **Windows**
   - **Type**: Double-hung, energy-efficient
   - **Number**: 6 (2 on each side, 2 on front)

#### 5. **Doors**
   - **Main Entrance**: Single wooden door
   - **Interior Doors**: Hollow core doors

#### 6. **Utilities**
   - **Electrical**: Basic wiring, outlets in each room
   - **Plumbing**: Basic setup for kitchen and bathroom

#### 7. **Interior Layout**
   - **Rooms**: 
     - Living Area: 15 ft x 20 ft
     - Kitchen: 10 ft x 10 ft
     - Bathroom: 5 ft x 8 ft
     - Bedroom: 10 ft x 12 ft

#### 8. **Exterior**
   - **Siding**: Vinyl or wood
   - **Landscaping**: Minimal, native plants

#### 9. **Safety Features**
   - **Smoke Detectors**: Installed in each room
   - **Fire Extinguisher**: Located in the kitchen

#### 10. **Finishing Touches**
   - **Paint**: Neutral colors for walls
   - **Trim**: Simple baseboards and crown molding

### Note
Adjust dimensions and materials based on specific site conditions and local building codes.
