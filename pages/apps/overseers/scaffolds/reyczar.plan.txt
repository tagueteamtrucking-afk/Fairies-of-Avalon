**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Reyczar'**

1. **Objective:**
   - Define the purpose and goals of the character Reyczar.

2. **Character Overview:**
   - Name: Reyczar
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

3. **Physical Description:**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, silver with hints of green
   - Attire: Leaf-patterned dress, adorned with flowers

4. **Personality Traits:**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Mischievous
   - Wise beyond years

5. **Abilities:**
   - Nature manipulation (growth of plants, healing)
   - Flight
   - Illusion creation

6. **Backstory:**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Key events: 
     - Protecting the forest from dark forces
     - Forming alliances with other magical beings

7. **Goals and Motivations:**
   - Protect Avalon’s natural beauty
   - Maintain harmony among magical creatures
   - Discover her true potential

8. **Challenges:**
   - Confronting dark magic
   - Balancing her playful nature with responsibilities

9. **Supporting Characters:**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy
   - Friend: A mischievous sprite
   - Antagonist: A dark sorcerer threatening Avalon

10. **Plot Points:**
    - Initial conflict with dark forces
    - Journey to discover ancient magic
    - Climax: Battle to save Avalon

11. **Conclusion:**
    - Resolution of conflicts
    - Reyczar’s growth and acceptance of her role as a guardian

12. **Visual References:**
    - Inspirational images of fairies and nature

13. **Notes:**
    - Consider incorporating themes of environmentalism and friendship.
