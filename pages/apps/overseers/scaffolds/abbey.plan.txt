**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Clear and level ground
   - Lay a base of compacted gravel or sand

2. **Structure**
   - **Walls**
     - Use lightweight materials (e.g., wood or bamboo)
     - Design with open spaces for airflow
   - **Roof**
     - A-frame or dome shape for rain runoff
     - Use natural materials (e.g., thatch or leaves)

3. **Access**
   - Create a small entrance with a door or curtain
   - Include a pathway leading to the entrance

4. **Interior Layout**
   - Designate areas for:
     - Sleeping (small nook or hammock)
     - Relaxation (seating with cushions)
     - Storage (shelves or baskets)

5. **Exterior Features**
   - Incorporate natural elements (e.g., plants, stones)
   - Add decorative elements (e.g., fairy lights, wind chimes)

6. **Utilities**
   - Plan for minimal lighting (solar or fairy lights)
   - Water source (small bowl or natural spring)

7. **Sustainability**
   - Use eco-friendly materials
   - Implement rainwater collection if possible

8. **Landscaping**
   - Surround with flowers and herbs
   - Create a small garden area for herbs or vegetables

9. **Safety**
   - Ensure stability of structure
   - Check for potential hazards (sharp objects, unstable ground) 

10. **Maintenance**
    - Schedule regular checks for wear and tear
    - Plan for seasonal adjustments (e.g., insulation for winter)
