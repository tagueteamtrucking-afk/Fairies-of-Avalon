**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Nature-inspired dress with floral patterns

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Role: Guardian of the forest's flora and fauna
   - Key Event: Discovered her powers during a storm

4. **Powers and Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Flight: Capable of swift and agile movement
   - Healing: Can mend wounds of creatures and plants

5. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect the Avalon forest
   - Foster harmony between magical beings and nature
   - Explore the world beyond Avalon

6. **Challenges and Conflicts**
   - Threats from dark forces seeking to exploit the forest
   - Struggles with self-doubt about her abilities
   - Balancing her curiosity with her responsibilities

7. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: An elder fairy who guides her
   - Antagonist: A dark sorcerer aiming to corrupt Avalon

8. **Character Development**
   - Growth from playful curiosity to responsible guardian
   - Learning to embrace her powers and leadership role
   - Building confidence through overcoming challenges

9. **Conclusion**
   - Billie’s journey emphasizes the importance of nature and friendship
   - Aims to inspire others to protect their own environments
