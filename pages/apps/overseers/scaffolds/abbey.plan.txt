**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base dimensions: 10m x 10m
   - Material: Reinforced concrete

2. **Vertical Structure**
   - Columns: 4 main support columns (wood or metal)
   - Height: 3m for main structure

3. **Roof**
   - Type: A-frame design
   - Material: Lightweight timber or thatch
   - Overhang: 1m on all sides

4. **Walls**
   - Material: Natural wood panels or woven branches
   - Height: 2.5m
   - Openings: 2 large windows (1m x 1.5m) for natural light

5. **Access**
   - Entrance: Single door (1m x 2m)
   - Steps: 3-step wooden staircase

6. **Interior Layout**
   - Open floor plan
   - Designated areas for seating and storage

7. **Exterior Features**
   - Garden space surrounding the structure
   - Pathway: Gravel or stone leading to the entrance

8. **Safety Measures**
   - Secure all structural joints
   - Regular inspections for stability

9. **Sustainability Considerations**
   - Use of eco-friendly materials
   - Rainwater collection system for garden irrigation

10. **Timeline**
    - Estimated construction duration: 4 weeks

**End of Plan**
