**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Establish a stable base using lightweight materials.
   - Ensure drainage and support for vertical structures.

2. **Vertical Structure**
   - Create a central tower with three levels:
     - Ground Level: Entryway and common area.
     - Middle Level: Living quarters for fairies.
     - Top Level: Observation deck with panoramic views.

3. **Horizontal Elements**
   - Connect levels with spiral staircases made of natural materials.
   - Include balconies on each level for outdoor access.

4. **Roofing**
   - Design a thatched roof for aesthetic appeal and insulation.
   - Incorporate skylights for natural light.

5. **Walls**
   - Use woven branches and leaves for walls, allowing for ventilation.
   - Integrate magical barriers for protection.

6. **Interior Layout**
   - Open floor plan for communal areas.
   - Designate private nooks for individual fairies.

7. **Landscaping**
   - Surround the structure with enchanted gardens.
   - Create pathways using stones and natural materials.

8. **Utilities**
   - Install a natural water source and purification system.
   - Use bioluminescent plants for lighting.

9. **Sustainability Features**
   - Implement rainwater harvesting.
   - Use renewable energy sources (e.g., solar panels disguised as leaves).

10. **Accessibility**
    - Ensure pathways are accessible for all fairy inhabitants.
    - Include magical portals for quick travel between areas.
