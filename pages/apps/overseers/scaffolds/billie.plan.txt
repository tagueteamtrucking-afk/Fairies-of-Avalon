**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Avalon Forest

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling butterfly wings
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leafy dress adorned with flowers

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Flight: Can fly gracefully through the forest
   - Nature Manipulation: Ability to encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Healing: Can heal small wounds using forest magic

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from a magical flower in Avalon
   - Mission: To protect the forest and its inhabitants from threats

6. **Goals**
   - Maintain harmony in Avalon
   - Educate others about the importance of nature
   - Find a way to restore balance when the forest is threatened

7. **Challenges**
   - Encounters with dark forces seeking to exploit the forest
   - Balancing her playful nature with the seriousness of her responsibilities

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy who guides Billie
   - Friends: A group of woodland creatures who assist her

9. **Setting**
   - Avalon Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical flora and fauna

10. **Plot Hooks**
    - Discovery of a new threat to the forest
    - A quest to find a lost magical artifact
    - A festival celebrating the forest’s magic that is interrupted by dark forces

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Billie'.
