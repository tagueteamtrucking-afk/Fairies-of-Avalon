**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Establish a foundational structure for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' to support development and growth.

2. **Key Components**
   - **Vision Statement**
     - Define the overarching goal and purpose of Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'.

   - **Target Audience**
     - Identify primary users and stakeholders.

   - **Core Features**
     - List essential features and functionalities.

3. **Development Phases**
   - **Phase 1: Research and Planning**
     - Conduct market analysis.
     - Gather user feedback.

   - **Phase 2: Design**
     - Create wireframes and prototypes.
     - Develop user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) guidelines.

   - **Phase 3: Implementation**
     - Build core features.
     - Integrate necessary technologies.

   - **Phase 4: Testing**
     - Perform usability testing.
     - Address bugs and feedback.

   - **Phase 5: Launch**
     - Prepare marketing materials.
     - Execute launch strategy.

4. **Timeline**
   - Outline a timeline for each phase with milestones.

5. **Budget**
   - Estimate costs associated with development, marketing, and maintenance.

6. **Evaluation**
   - Define metrics for success and methods for ongoing assessment.

7. **Maintenance Plan**
   - Establish a schedule for updates and user support.
