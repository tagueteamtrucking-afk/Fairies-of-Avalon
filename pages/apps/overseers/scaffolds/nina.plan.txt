**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Nina'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Nina
   - Type: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Gossamer, iridescent
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Ethereal dress made of flower petals and leaves

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Curious
   - Protective of nature

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth and communicate with animals
   - Flight: Capable of swift, agile flight
   - Healing: Possesses minor healing abilities for flora and fauna

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted woods of Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of the forest and its creatures
   - Quest: To restore balance to a threatened part of her realm

6. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest animals (e.g., rabbits, birds)

7. **Conflict**
   - Antagonist: A dark sorcerer threatening Avalon’s magic
   - Challenge: Must gather allies and harness her powers to confront the sorcerer

8. **Goals**
   - Protect Avalon’s magic
   - Restore harmony in her realm
   - Discover her true potential as a fairy

9. **Visual Style**
   - Color Palette: Soft pastels with hints of sparkle
   - Art Style: Whimsical and enchanting

10. **Conclusion**
    - Nina’s journey emphasizes themes of courage, friendship, and environmental stewardship.
