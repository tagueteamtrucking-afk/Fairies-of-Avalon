**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Curious
   - Playful
   - Wise

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight
   - Invisibility

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the essence of a sacred tree in Avalon
   - Mission: Protect the balance of nature and assist lost creatures

6. **Allies and Relationships**
   - Friends: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: Elder fairy of Avalon

7. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Educate others about nature's importance

8. **Challenges**
   - Threats from dark forces seeking to corrupt Avalon
   - Balancing her playful nature with her responsibilities

9. **Development Arc**
   - Growth from a carefree fairy to a wise guardian
   - Learning to harness her powers more effectively

10. **Conclusion**
    - Clarice's journey emphasizes the importance of harmony with nature and the power of friendship.
