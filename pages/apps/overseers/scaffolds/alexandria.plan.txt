**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria'**

1. **Objective**
   - Create a lightweight and flexible scaffold for Avalon Fairy 'Alexandria' to enhance gameplay and storytelling.

2. **Core Elements**
   - **Character Design**
     - Define Alexandria's appearance, abilities, and personality traits.
   - **Backstory**
     - Develop a brief history that explains Alexandria's origins and motivations.
   - **Abilities**
     - List unique powers or skills (e.g., nature manipulation, healing).
   - **Goals**
     - Outline primary objectives for the character within the narrative.

3. **Environment**
   - **Setting**
     - Describe the primary locations where Alexandria will interact (e.g., enchanted forest, mystical glade).
   - **Key NPCs**
     - Identify important non-player characters that will influence Alexandria's journey.

4. **Gameplay Mechanics**
   - **Interactions**
     - Define how players will interact with Alexandria (e.g., quests, dialogue options).
   - **Progression**
     - Outline a simple leveling system or skill enhancement related to Alexandria's abilities.

5. **Narrative Structure**
   - **Plot Points**
     - Identify major plot points or events that will involve Alexandria.
   - **Conflict**
     - Establish the main conflict or challenge Alexandria must face.

6. **Visual and Audio Elements**
   - **Art Style**
     - Specify the visual aesthetic for Alexandria and her surroundings.
   - **Sound Design**
     - Consider ambient sounds and music that enhance the fairy's magical theme.

7. **Testing and Feedback**
   - Plan for playtesting sessions to gather feedback on Alexandria's character and gameplay integration.

8. **Iteration**
   - Schedule regular reviews to refine and enhance Alexandria based on player feedback and gameplay experience.
