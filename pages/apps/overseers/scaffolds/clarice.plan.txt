**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-themed dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight
   - Invisibility

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from a sacred flower in Avalon
   - Mission: Protect the balance of nature and assist lost creatures

6. **Goals**
   - Maintain harmony in Avalon
   - Help others discover their true potential

7. **Challenges**
   - Dark forces threatening Avalon
   - Balancing her playful nature with responsibilities

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest creatures

9. **Story Arc**
   - Introduction: Clarice's daily life in Avalon
   - Conflict: Encounter with dark forces
   - Resolution: Overcoming challenges and restoring balance

10. **Visual Style**
    - Color Palette: Soft pastels, greens, and earth tones
    - Artistic Influence: Whimsical and ethereal designs

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the character Clarice within the Avalon Fairy narrative.
