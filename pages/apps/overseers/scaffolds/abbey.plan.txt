**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Abbey'**

1. **Foundation**
   - Level ground preparation
   - Base support structure

2. **Main Structure**
   - Vertical supports (4 corners)
   - Horizontal beams (top and mid-level)

3. **Walls**
   - Lightweight panels (wood or fabric)
   - Openings for windows and doors

4. **Roof**
   - Sloped design for rain runoff
   - Lightweight covering (thatched or fabric)

5. **Interior Layout**
   - Designated areas (living, sleeping, storage)
   - Minimal furnishings (bed, table, seating)

6. **Exterior Features**
   - Garden space (flower beds, pathways)
   - Decorative elements (fairy lights, sculptures)

7. **Access**
   - Ladder or stairs for entry
   - Pathway leading to the entrance

8. **Utilities (if applicable)**
   - Basic lighting (solar or battery)
   - Water source (if needed)

9. **Safety Measures**
   - Stability checks on all structures
   - Secure all elements against wind and weather

10. **Final Touches**
    - Paint or finish as desired
    - Add personal fairy touches (artifacts, charms) 

**Note:** Ensure all materials are eco-friendly and suitable for the environment.
