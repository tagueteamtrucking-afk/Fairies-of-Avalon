**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years
   - Curious about the human world

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight
   - Invisibility
   - Communication with animals

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Purpose: To protect the balance of nature and guide lost creatures

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Help lost souls find their way
   - Foster harmony between fairies and nature

7. **Allies and Relationships**
   - Friends: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Mentor: Elder fairy who taught her about nature's secrets
   - Rival: A mischievous sprite who disrupts her work

8. **Challenges and Conflicts**
   - Threats to Avalon’s environment
   - Misunderstandings with humans
   - Balancing her duties with personal desires

9. **Story Arc**
   - Introduction: Clarice discovers a disturbance in Avalon
   - Rising Action: Encounters challenges and meets new allies
   - Climax: Confrontation with the source of the disturbance
   - Resolution: Restores balance and learns valuable lessons

10. **Visual and Thematic Elements**
    - Color Palette: Soft pastels with vibrant greens
    - Themes: Nature, friendship, balance, and growth

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'.
