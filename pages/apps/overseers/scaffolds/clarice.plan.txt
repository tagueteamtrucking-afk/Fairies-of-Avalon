**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Clarice'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Clarice
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Appearance**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Leaf and flower-inspired dress

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Playful
   - Wise beyond her years
   - Curious about the human world

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can accelerate plant growth and communicate with animals.
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal minor wounds and ailments.
   - Flight: Capable of swift and agile movement through the air.

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon.
   - Purpose: Tasked with maintaining the balance of nature and protecting the realm from dark forces.

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - Protect Avalon’s natural beauty.
   - Foster harmony between magical creatures and humans.
   - Discover the secrets of ancient magic.

7. **Challenges**
   - Encounters with dark creatures threatening Avalon.
   - Struggles with self-doubt about her abilities.
   - Navigating the complexities of human interactions.

8. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon.
   - Forest animals.
   - A wise old tree spirit.

9. **Conflict**
   - Main antagonist: A dark sorceress seeking to corrupt Avalon.
   - Internal conflict: Balancing her duties with her desire for adventure.

10. **Resolution**
    - Overcoming challenges through teamwork and courage.
    - Learning to embrace her unique gifts and strengths.
