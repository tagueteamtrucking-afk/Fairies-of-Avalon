**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, mischievous, kind-hearted

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Outfit: Nature-inspired, flower motifs

3. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the enchanted Avalon forest
   - Role: Guardian of nature and harmony
   - Motivation: Protect the balance of the magical realm

4. **Abilities**
   - Magic: Nature manipulation (growth, healing)
   - Flight: Swift and agile in the air
   - Communication: Can speak to animals and plants

5. **Goals**
   - Short-term: Help a lost creature find its way home
   - Long-term: Preserve the Avalon forest from dark forces

6. **Relationships**
   - Allies: Other fairies, woodland creatures
   - Rivals: Dark spirits threatening the forest

7. **Challenges**
   - Overcoming her mischievous nature to focus on responsibilities
   - Battling dark forces that disrupt the harmony of Avalon

8. **Development Arc**
   - Growth from playful trickster to a responsible guardian
   - Learning the importance of teamwork and trust

9. **Conclusion**
   - Resolution of personal conflicts and restoration of balance in Avalon.
