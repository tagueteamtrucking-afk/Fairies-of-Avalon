**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Reyczar'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Reyczar
   - Species: Fairy
   - Realm: Avalon
   - Role: Guardian of Nature

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wing Type: Gossamer, iridescent
   - Hair: Silver with hints of blue
   - Eyes: Emerald green
   - Attire: Leaf-patterned dress, adorned with dew drops

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Curious
   - Playful
   - Protective of the environment
   - Wise beyond her years

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Flight: Agile and swift in the air
   - Healing: Can mend wounds of flora and fauna

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring in Avalon
   - Mission: To maintain balance in nature and protect her realm from dark forces

6. **Allies**
   - Other fairies of Avalon
   - Forest creatures (e.g., deer, birds)
   - Elemental spirits

7. **Antagonists**
   - Dark sorcerers seeking to corrupt Avalon
   - Pollution and environmental threats

8. **Goals**
   - Preserve the beauty of Avalon
   - Educate others about the importance of nature
   - Defend her realm from external threats

9. **Key Locations**
   - Enchanted Forest: Home and sanctuary
   - Crystal Lake: Source of her healing powers
   - Ancient Oak: Meeting place for fairy council

10. **Plot Hooks**
    - Discovery of a dark force threatening Avalon
    - Quest to find a lost artifact that can restore balance
    - Journey to unite different fairy clans against a common enemy

This scaffold provides a foundational outline for the character Reyczar, allowing for further development and storytelling.
