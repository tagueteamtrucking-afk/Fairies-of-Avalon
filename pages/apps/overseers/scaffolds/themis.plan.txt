**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Themis'**

1. **Objective**
   - Create a character design and backstory for Avalon Fairy 'Themis'.

2. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Themis
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of Justice and Balance

3. **Physical Appearance**
   - Wings: Iridescent, resembling stained glass
   - Hair: Silver with hints of blue
   - Attire: Flowing gown in shades of white and blue, adorned with symbols of scales

4. **Personality Traits**
   - Wise
   - Fair-minded
   - Compassionate

5. **Abilities**
   - Power to restore balance in nature
   - Ability to influence decisions towards fairness
   - Healing touch

6. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first light of dawn in Avalon
   - Purpose: To maintain harmony among the fairy realms and protect the innocent

7. **Allies and Enemies**
   - Allies: Other Avalon Fairies, Nature Spirits
   - Enemies: Dark entities that disrupt balance

8. **Goals and Motivations**
   - To uphold justice in the fairy realms
   - To teach others the importance of balance and fairness

9. **Conflict**
   - Struggles against forces that seek to create chaos and injustice

10. **Conclusion**
   - Themis serves as a beacon of hope and fairness in the magical world of Avalon.
