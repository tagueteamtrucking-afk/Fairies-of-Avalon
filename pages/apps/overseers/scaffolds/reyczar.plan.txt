**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Reyczar'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Reyczar
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Role: Guardian of the Enchanted Forest

2. **Physical Attributes**
   - Height: 5 feet
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, silver with hints of blue
   - Attire: Ethereal gown made of leaves and flowers

3. **Personality Traits**
   - Kind-hearted
   - Mischievous
   - Wise beyond years

4. **Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Control over plants and animals
   - Healing Touch: Ability to heal minor wounds
   - Illusion Crafting: Create illusions to protect the forest

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born from the first bloom of spring
   - Purpose: To maintain balance in the forest and protect its secrets

6. **Allies**
   - Forest Creatures: Birds, deer, and magical beings
   - Other Fairies: Collaborates with fellow fairies for forest protection

7. **Challenges**
   - Threats: Encroachment by humans and dark magic
   - Internal Conflict: Balancing mischief with responsibility

8. **Goals**
   - Protect the Enchanted Forest
   - Foster harmony between nature and humans

9. **Visual References**
   - Color Palette: Greens, blues, and silvers
   - Inspiration: Nature, fantasy art, and folklore

10. **Narrative Arc**
    - Introduction: Reyczar’s daily life in the forest
    - Conflict: Encounter with a human threatening the forest
    - Resolution: Reyczar’s clever plan to restore balance

This scaffold serves as a foundational outline for developing the character of Avalon Fairy 'Reyczar'.
