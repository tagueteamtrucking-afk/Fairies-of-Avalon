**Minimal Scaffold Plan for Avalon Fairy 'Billie'**

1. **Character Overview**
   - Name: Billie
   - Type: Avalon Fairy
   - Traits: Playful, curious, nurturing

2. **Physical Description**
   - Height: 5 inches
   - Wings: Iridescent, butterfly-like
   - Hair: Long, flowing, pastel colors
   - Attire: Floral dress, adorned with leaves and petals

3. **Magical Abilities**
   - Nature Manipulation: Can encourage plant growth
   - Light Manipulation: Creates soft glows to illuminate dark areas
   - Healing Touch: Can heal small wounds in animals and plants

4. **Personality Traits**
   - Friendly and approachable
   - Inquisitive about the world
   - Protective of her forest home

5. **Backstory**
   - Origin: Born in the heart of the Avalon forest
   - Role: Guardian of flora and fauna
   - Key Event: Saved a dying tree, earning the respect of other fairies

6. **Goals and Motivations**
   - To maintain harmony in her forest
   - To explore beyond her home and learn about other magical beings

7. **Challenges**
   - Threats from pollution and deforestation
   - Misunderstandings with humans

8. **Supporting Characters**
   - Mentor: Elder Fairy who guides her
   - Friends: A mischievous squirrel and a wise owl

9. **Setting**
   - Avalon Forest: Lush, vibrant, filled with magical creatures

10. **Plot Points**
    - Discovery of a new threat to her home
    - Journey to seek help from other magical beings
    - Resolution through collaboration and understanding

This scaffold provides a foundational structure for developing the character of Billie, the Avalon Fairy.
